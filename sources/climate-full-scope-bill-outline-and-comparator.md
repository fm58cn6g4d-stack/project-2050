October 23, 2026 · Drafting specification, not introduced bill text or an adopted merger. Source branch `claude/pensive-mendel-g1lwl5`, baseline `f159cdf01a8f3352478eaa74cf08c501376af06e`. Governing D2/D5/D7 and chosen CLI-3A/CLI-4A/CLI-6A control. Preserve CLI-7/8's separate full fallback and MOV-31's second-Congress placement.

A combined bill has a substantive case: the CES needs physical delivery capacity, and statutory climate authority needs the chosen permitting-law corrections. Whether consolidation improves passage and durability still depends on actual text, referrals, amendments and coalition. This outline makes the scope auditable before anyone treats fewer bill numbers as a saving.

## Combined outline

Local title numbers below are drafting labels, not new policy IDs. Each requires definitions, coverage, implementing authority, payment/enforcement rules, effective dates, reporting and review provisions consistent with its canonical design. Missing dollar caps, dates or engineering thresholds remain questions for drafting; this outline does not choose them.

Cross-cutting drafting must address severability, judicial review, agency capacity, transition and appropriations without inventing new protections that Espen has not chosen. Where canonical language leaves a material design open, return the choice with alternatives. An outline cannot establish legal durability merely by including a severability section.

## Separate comparator with identical coverage

**Bill A:** titles I–VI above, preserving the existing CLI-3/4 bundle. **Bill B:** titles VII–XII, preserving all CLI-6A components. Both require aligned effective dates, shared definitions where appropriate and coordinated implementation. Existing CLI-1/2/R2 and CLI-5/R3 funding/revenue are separate; the floor merger does not move or double-count them.

A hybrid may move supported provisions first only if every remaining chosen provision has an identified vehicle and reservation. CLI-6B's transfer/large-load subset is not a full grid-bill substitute. Neither savings nor bipartisan durability can be credited before the residual scope and coalition are known.

## Referral, amendments and passage review

Use coordinated Senate ENR/EPW and House Energy and Commerce work, with transportation/water and other jurisdiction consultations based on the final text. Formal referral is a parliamentary determination. Obtain a title-to-committee map in each chamber rather than treating “energy bill” as single-committee jurisdiction.

Keep D2-F's ten amendments per side and equal time. Prepare an amendment inventory covering weakening the CES/CAA authority, restoring uncapped damages, narrowing water corrections, stripping utility accountability, exempting existing loads and delaying cyber compliance. These are plausible lines of contention inferred from the text, not identified senators' positions. For each, state the chosen position, interaction with other titles, scoring and the consequence of defeat. Do not manufacture a whip count from general bipartisan interest in transmission.
