## Accounting choices

- **Credit split:** the pricing denominator includes $0.90T of CLI-1 and $0.22T of CLI-5 ITC/45X. Allocate the same $178.5B conservative saving proportionally: $143.4B and $35.1B. CLI-1 is consequently $0.757T, not the unreconciled ~$0.72T sketch; CLI-5 gross spending is $0.565T. No second overlap saving is taken. A provision-level joint score remains necessary.
- **Program rows:** CLI-5W is $67.5B in 2025 dollars over 2031–39 ($84.0B nominal); CLI-10G is the lean grid expected deficit cost ($39.4B), not loan principal. CLI-9 is $109.5B, CLI-13F forests $24.6B and CLI-13A farms $30.8B. CLI-7 permanent disaster pieces, CLI-7N NFIP resolution and CLI-7K capital are disjoint. Standards have zero direct outlay; clean heat starts in 2032.
- **Receipts and O5:** C1P separately books $244.3B process receipts; CB1 books the $141.6B reduction in combustion receipts. CXP is the 25% net excise offset ($25.7B cost), on top of unchanged baseline CX. CLI-2 includes the incremental dividend; CLI-2O records incremental receipts minus that dividend as financing of gross program rows. The accounting identity is gross GF items + CX + channel uses + dividend − added receipts = net GF cost. D4-B capital is added once, separately. The baseline 20% share remains credited once to CLI-5; none of the $150 step is reused.
- **Reinsurance:** CLI-7C scores expected net cost, $0.3B per year in 2025 dollars from FY2031 (range −0.3 to +1.0), $3.36B nominal through 2039. The $75–150B contingent exposure appears in D4 records but is not an additional expected outlay. Tail losses and recoupment incidence remain risks.
- **Capital:** C44 classifies $151.9B of already-booked capital as D4-B borrowing; it is not a saving or an expansion of the envelope. The recovery surge reduction previously freed about $195B: do not credit that saving again. Central/conservative capital uses about $152B, leaving about $43B of that increment; unfavorable capital about $212B exceeds it. DEC retains the historical larger surge and fails maturity, so its capital classification is not a declaration of available borrowing authority.
- **Long term:** the export ends in 2050. For 2051–56, hold each 2050 real flow constant at the export’s 2.2% inflation rate; ended programs stay ended. This homogeneous extension preserves the O5 identities but is not a new emissions/revenue forecast. No post-2050 fiscal gain from faster abatement is assumed.

## Household limits and next assignment

The household diagnostic includes the process fee with a full-pass-through consumption proxy, lost combustion fees with the energy key, the actual annual O5 dividend, reduced household credits, and retrofit/bill support with the low-income-energy key. Retrofit spending is an in-kind benefit proxy, not cash. Disaster risk reduction, industrial spillovers, additional system-price effects, within-quintile variation and tail recoupment are not established by these averages. Scenario Q is a ten-year average; the mixed result is FY2039 and uses different financing. Neither establishes D3.

**Next bounded scope:** a joint revenue settlement and fourth-quintile protection comparison, owned by the common fiscal integrator. Reconcile G1/G5/G7 and corporate C38 against current law; then price two or three worker/nonworker refundable protection schedules against those net receipts. Test annual household cells (including disability, retirement and self-employment), early affordability, D4 maturity and downside debt without spending the same revenue twice. Return one comparison PR with explicit unresolved ranges; reserve any strategic downsizing decision for Espen. Do not open new program expansions in that task.
