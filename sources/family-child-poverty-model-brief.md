## Model design

Use a named recent CPS ASEC/SPM vintage with documented aging/reweighting to 2030 and a mature-service year (2033–34). Build a post-2025 current-law counterfactual. Preserve tax units and SPM resource-sharing units separately; document imputation, survey exclusions and benefit-underreporting corrections.

Calculate taxes, refundable credits, relevant transfers, medical/work/care expenses and geographic/housing-tenure thresholds jointly. Test statutory full receipt, plausible delivered receipt and enhanced-delivery cases. Model subgroup differences rather than a flat national take-up haircut. Keep employment responses as explicit no-response and supported sensitivity cases; care-induced employment changes are a separate channel. No dynamic revenue is inserted into D4.

Report annual SPM, deep poverty, near poverty, poverty-gap dollars and counts by age, family structure/size, earnings, disability, identification/citizenship status, race/ethnicity, geography and tenure. Report monthly liquidity/hardship separately and disclose unreliable small cells. A higher annual income does not establish a timely payment or a usable service.

## Residual attribution without double counting

First report overlapping mechanism flags for each child: legal exclusion, eligible nonreceipt, resource inadequacy after receipt, and unavailable care/food/housing service. Then, only if a mutually exclusive table is useful, apply a declared order (exclusion, nonreceipt, remaining income inadequacy, other service failure, no recorded mechanism) and publish overlap cross-tabs alongside it. The order changes attribution, not the underlying child count. Never add overlapping mechanism counts or separate policy-effect percentages.

## Comparison and validation plan

Start with adopted rules and delivery improvements. Compare explicit eligibility branches, FAM-1D, HOU-4C, care/summer-delivery improvements and an illustrative residual supplement only as alternatives. Do not assign an undefined supplement a fabricated amount or adopt it through a scenario name. Price permanent changes and retain D3/D4 constraints.

Validate tax/SPM identities and a feasible published comparator, explaining differences in population, baseline, parameters and receipt. Keep code/data provenance, an assumptions table, uncertainty and reproducible output tables. A licensed/proprietary model is not presumed available; identify access and skills needed before promising execution.
