# Project 2050: one Medicaid-renewal-to-registration transaction

October 23, 2026 · DEM-1E follow-up for Espen Swanson

**Status: proposed service specification and illustrative cost comparison.** This is a worked transaction, not a deployed system, approved budget or change to adopted DEM-1E. It compares a minimum viable 2030 assisted service, a conditional Oregon automatic route, and broader rollout. All new service targets, staffing quantities, prices and remedy additions are proposals or illustrative assumptions. No adopted policy, model input or date was changed.

## Finding and proposed path

The minimum service should let an eligible adult complete a registration application, receive assistance, track its delivery and correct an error during a Medicaid renewal contact, without making the health transaction depend on the election transaction. It can use the official online form or a signed paper form. A link alone is insufficient: the public-assistance agency's existing acceptance, assistance and transmittal duties still matter. [L1]

Oregon already has a more ambitious statutory route. ORS 247.018 provides for OHP-derived electronic records, subject to applicable federal law, verified transfer processes and implementing rules. Its stated operative date is June 1, 2026. This is evidence of state authority, **not proof that CMS authorization, rules, contracts and production service are all in place**. An actual approval and operating record were not located in this review. [L3]

**Proposed development sequence:** price and test the assisted service as the 2030 floor; in parallel establish whether Oregon's existing automatic route can lawfully operate, and use it if the required approvals and service safeguards are demonstrated. Present a nationwide automatic rollout as a separately legislated and funded expansion. An assisted-only 2030 bridge falls short of DEM-1E's automatic default and universal reach; Espen must approve any adoption of that staging. It is not silently substituted here.

This is not a choice between doing nothing and a new system. NVRA assistance is already a legal baseline in covered public-assistance offices. The incremental proposal is reliable completion and correction, with new automatic functionality only where its authority and safeguards are established. Price baseline compliance gaps separately from genuinely new statutory duties.

## 3. Lawful fields and adult eligibility

**Worked case, wholly fictional:** a 34-year-old naturalized U.S. citizen, resident in Oregon, responds to an OHP renewal request and updates a residential address. Their household includes a noncitizen spouse and two children. The adult has no Oregon driver license but can provide the last four SSN digits and an image of their handwritten signature through the official election form. The example assumes no other election-law disqualification. It is not an assertion about any real person or caseload.

Use one adult's record and action. A parent's citizenship, a Medicaid approval, a benefits representative's authority or a household signature does not establish every household member's electoral eligibility. Immigration eligibility for health coverage is not U.S. citizenship. An SSN is an identifier, not citizenship proof. A nonmatch is not a finding of noncitizenship.

| Field / record | Assisted transaction | Conditional automatic route | Restriction / correction rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Legal name, date of birth/age | Adult enters it in the official election form | Transfer only the approved equivalent with individual provenance | Names and addresses are protected Medicaid information too; ordinary identity data are not freely exportable. Correct the appropriate record, preserving prior versions. |
| Voting residence and separate mailing address | Adult confirms the actual voting residence and a different mailing address if needed | Only the authorized residence fields; validate safe-address handling before transfer | Household mailing address may not be voting residence. Homelessness and confidential-address cases need an election specialist; no health-address overwrite. |
| U.S. citizenship | Adult's election attestation under the applicable form | A lawful, positive, individual-level source meeting state rules; authority for reuse must be documented | Do not transmit a known noncitizen's record through ORS 247.018. An unverified or conflicting record goes to assistance, not automatic submission or an enforcement referral. |
| Age / adult status | Worked service is 18+; show the separate lawful youth route where relevant | Apply approved individual qualification rules | Oregon permits registration at 16; voting begins at 18. An adult-only pilot must not remove youth registration opportunities or existing agency duties. |
| Driver/ID identifier, last four SSN digits, or lawful alternative | Use only what the chosen official form requires; the paper form supplies a no-ID/no-SSN alternative | Verify the approved payload; do not assume these identifiers belong in the OHA transfer | Never export the Medicaid full SSN to simplify matching. Preserve a lawful route for someone without the chosen identifier. |
| Signature and election attestation | Separate election signature; no reuse of the Medicaid renewal signature as consent | ORS 247.018 allows the described registration record without a signature, but requires a signature record before a ballot can count | A voice assent or benefits e-signature is not automatically the verifiable handwritten-signature image required by that statute. Track signature cure separately from registered status. |
| Party, phone and email | Let the adult make any party selection; do not infer it. Contact details only as permitted/selected by the adult | Party choice through the lawful notice process, without agency assignment | Neither health staff nor vendor recommends a party. Separate required form fields from optional contact information. |
| Prior registration and correction history | Supply relevant previous details on the official form | Use an authorized deduplication process and retain evidence of the resolution | No automatic cancellation from one name/address mismatch or a Medicaid termination. |
| Medical, income, tax, benefit amount, immigration-document and household-member records | Excluded from the election payload | Excluded except any strictly necessary citizenship fact expressly authorized for this purpose | No diagnosis, full case file, child/spouse roster, IRS return information or raw verification response. An agreement alone cannot override source restrictions. |
| Agency source, decline and audit records | Restricted operational record for registration purposes | Same, with legally required provenance | The public voter file must not disclose the particular registration agency. Decline information has restricted use; no benefits decision, party targeting or public person-level dashboard. |

The election-field specification draws on the official Oregon form, the expanded online route and state law. It does not authorize an integrated Medicaid export. Federal Medicaid safeguards cover names, addresses, SSNs and eligibility-verification information; the reviewed regulation recognizes NVRA materials but does not itself authorize every automatic data transfer. [L2–L6]

**Two lawful routes must remain distinct.** In the assisted service, the adult completes a separate election application directly; use deliberate entry or confirmation on that service, not a hidden Medicaid prefill. Counsel must approve any prefill, session transfer or signature reuse. In the automatic service, source reuse depends on the actual federal/state legal basis, rules, permitted evidence and approved operating procedure. Ordinary permission to disclose under 42 CFR 431.306 is not assumed to erase all purpose limits, IRS restrictions or partner-agency conditions.

The specific 2029–30 renewal cadence, health eligibility rules and identity/citizenship procedures must be refreshed against enacted law and state authority before launch. This example does not assume that every adult renews annually or that future HLT-1 repeal has already occurred.

## 4. One transaction, from renewal to confirmed disposition

The following is a proposed operating specification for the fictional adult's **assisted** transaction. It preserves the automatic route as a parallel alternative rather than inventing its missing approvals.

| Step | Action and owner to designate | Evidence retained / outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Renewal and election opportunity** | OHA/ODHS presents required registration materials and help with the renewal. The health submission can complete independently. | Renewal receipt and required health notice. Election offer available in accessible, translated and assisted channels; no forced health delay. |
| **2. Adult chooses** | Adult elects to apply, declines, or does not act. Staff explains that health eligibility/benefits do not depend on the choice. | Required confidential decline record where applicable. Silence does not become an assisted application; statutory form distribution and assistance rules still apply. |
| **3. Separate election form** | Adult opens the official election service or receives a paper form. Staff offers the same degree of help required by NVRA; adult can complete privately. | Election information collected for election purposes. The example uses last-four-SSN and a signature image; paper remains available. No household file moves with it. |
| **4. Review and signature** | Adult checks citizenship, personal details, residence and party choice, reads notices and signs the election application. | Version of the official form and attestation; confirmation of what was submitted. Staff does not infer or sign on the adult's behalf without valid election authority. |
| **5. Acceptance and delivery** | Designated agency accepts a completed application for transmittal; direct official online submission goes to election officials through that official service. | Time of agency acceptance or official submission; destination, transport result and application reference. A local “saved” message is not proof of election receipt. |
| **6. County decision** | County election official checks qualification, duplicates and completeness and sends the required disposition notice. | Separate states for received, needs correction, accepted and rejected; a reason and correction route. Application count is not registration count. |
| **7. Error and cure** | Adult notices the wrong apartment number. Election service accepts correction, identifies any deadline risk and obtains the county's disposition. Medicaid address changes separately if needed. | Original/corrected values, submission times and confirmation. No silent substitution of a household mailing address or erasure of the first timely receipt. |
| **8. Completion and follow-up** | Adult receives confirmation and can check election status through an official channel. Service owner monitors unresolved cases and funded support. | Closed only after a substantive disposition or a clearly recorded unresolved case; optional survey does not control assistance. |

Oregon announced an online route using last-four-SSN and a valid signature on December 30, 2025. Do not repeat an older assumption that a DMV credential is always required online. Availability in the announcement is not a load test, approval of a third-party API or proof of an integrated ONE transaction. [L4]

**Timing and receipts.** Under NVRA §7(d), agency-accepted applications generally must be transmitted within 10 days, or within 5 days if accepted in the five days before the registration deadline. The proposed operating goal is same-day delivery and next-business-day exception review, with faster intervention near a deadline; these are service targets, not changes in law. For a valid timely agency application, 52 U.S.C. §20507(a)(1)(C) makes agency acceptance consequential. Preserve that timestamp and seek legal relief for wrongful delay; do not tell an applicant that the agency's later failure necessarily destroys their timely application. [L1, L7]

For a November election, the service must calculate the actual year's deadline using the current state rule, time zone, channel and applicable exceptions. The current Oregon form gives a 21-days-before-election registration deadline. DEM-1 proposes same-day registration; it is not current Oregon law merely because it appears in the project. [L5]

**Ex parte renewals.** Preserve renewals that can be completed from reliable information without requiring a beneficiary response. Do not force someone to log in, attest again or answer a voting question to retain coverage. Provide the election opportunity through legally appropriate notices or voluntary contacts without turning it into a new health condition. The sample intentionally uses a renewal requiring a response. [L8]

## 5. Decline, errors and effective remedies

| Event | Service behavior | Existing legal route / proposed addition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adult declines or leaves assisted choice unanswered | Complete health processing; no election submission based on silence. Explain how to register later. No repeated pressure or partisan follow-up. | NVRA's form and assistance rules control. Add confidential preference handling that does not suppress future legally required offers. |
| Adult says citizenship is uncertain or record conflicts | Stop automatic eligibility inference; provide private election assistance and a lawful route to establish/correct the relevant fact. | No extra voting-document condition imposed by a health worker. Proposed safe harbor and no-adverse-health rule require express drafting; no immunity promise under present law. |
| Known noncitizen household member | No record transferred under the OHA automatic route. Do not treat the household's other eligible adult as ineligible. | ORS 247.018(1)(c) is explicit. The assisted service does not prepare a false citizenship attestation. |
| Transmission fails or county receipt is absent | Keep the health receipt valid; use an encrypted queue, deduplication and human escalation. Tell the adult what remains unresolved and offer lawful paper/direct submission. | Preserve timely agency acceptance evidence. Seek an expedited election remedy where available; no assurance of acceptance or ballot counting. |
| Wrong address / duplicate / no signature | Explain the specific issue, accept correction through an authorized channel and confirm the county's action. | Apply state election correction and list-maintenance law. Do not cancel a registration automatically because the Medicaid record changes. |
| Automatic opt-out notice undeliverable | Investigate safe contact and stop treating a silent failed delivery as demonstrated informed choice. | Counsel must reconcile this safeguard with the actual statutory process. A paused transfer must not erase an existing lawful registration or substitute for a required notice. |
| Agency mistakenly registers an ineligible person | Secure and preserve evidence; promptly involve election counsel and correct through lawful individual procedures, with notice and assistance. | Proposed protection for people reasonably relying on government error needs explicit law and immigration/criminal-law review. Registration, voting and false statements have distinct consequences; no blanket present immunity. |
| Staff ties benefit to voting choice, or repeated service failure | Immediate supervisory correction and separate health review; register the NVRA complaint and offer a private route to help. | DOJ may sue for declaratory/injunctive relief. An aggrieved person has the statutory notice-and-cure/private-action route described below. No automatic damages entitlement is assumed. [L9] |
| Health coverage is wrongly denied | Health notice, correction, fair-hearing and any applicable continuation procedure run separately. | Election correction does not cure lost coverage; existing continuation rules have conditions. Obtain Medicaid counsel for the actual adverse action. |

Under 52 U.S.C. §20510, private enforcement ordinarily follows notice to the state's chief election official and an uncorrected violation after 90 days; the interval is 20 days for violations within 120 days before a federal election, and notice is unnecessary for violations within 30 days. Counsel should assess the actual facts and available urgent relief. A complaint button is useful but cannot guarantee a timely court order. [L9]

**Automatic-route practical defect to solve:** ORS 247.018 provides a 21-day post-notice period and allows a completed registration record without a signature, but directs rules ensuring no ballot counts without an electronic signature. A dashboard that celebrates registration while leaving people unable to have their ballots counted would reward the wrong outcome. The proposed service funds signature retrieval, accessible capture, timely notice and cure, and measures signature-complete readiness separately. The bill's national design must address analogous gaps rather than equate registration with access to a counted ballot. [L3; proposal]

| Federal source will not permit citizenship-data reuse | Keep the assisted attestation route within applicable election law; do not export restricted verification responses | Resolve source-specific legislation/permissions and contestability. No universal SSA, SAVE, tax or Hub query is assumed available. |
| State not subject to the NVRA on the same basis | Do not apply the covered-state model mechanically | Identify exemptions and applicable state/election law; the proposed national statute must specify its coverage and authority. |

A broader statute should separately address the Elections Clause route for congressional elections, presidential-election authority and state/local coverage. Congress's power to regulate election procedure is not an unrestricted power to redefine voter qualifications. These questions, and any anti-commandeering, spending-condition or immunity issue, require election and constitutional counsel. The minimum assisted workflow does not resolve them by relying on Medicaid's funding relationship.

## 8. Readiness, measurement and correction incentives

**2030 gates, proposed rather than promised dates:** legal/data permission; accountable program and county owners; funded signed scope; tested renewal independence and election delivery; accessibility/language testing with intended users; accurate qualification and opt-out handling; staffed correction; and successful deadline/outage rehearsals. **Proposed operating rule:** a daily supervisor reviews unacknowledged applications and near-deadline errors; systemic failures go to the named service owner and county lead immediately. An independent evaluator publishes aggregate results and missingness. The owner answers a material adverse finding within an illustrative 30 days with an affected-case search, funded correction plan and follow-up date. Evaluator publication and access must be protected through actual authority and contracts. Concealment or failure to correct has consequences; honest reporting of defects should not reduce a site's grant merely because it exposes more errors.

Do not randomize away legally required assistance to create a control group. Evaluation can compare phased **additional** support, with consent and an appropriate design, or use independent audit and before/after measures with candid causal limits. Record de-identified operational counts where feasible; any linkage for research needs its own purpose, access and retention review. Public reports must not expose benefit use, decline or the agency through which a person registered.

## 10. Public explanation

When you renew health coverage, you should be able to apply to register to vote or update your registration in the same contact. Your health application must proceed whether you register, decline or ask for help. The election application is a separate decision, and election officials determine its outcome.

In this proposed 2030 service, you could use an official online form, a paper form or assistance from a trained person. You would receive a receipt and a way to correct mistakes. A receipt means the application was submitted or accepted for delivery; the election office's notice tells you its disposition. Some voter-registration information is public, while the agency where you registered and your decision to decline receive legal protection.

Project 2050 also proposes automatic registration for eligible people at benefit contacts. Oregon has already legislated a conditional OHP route. Before relying on it, the responsible agencies must demonstrate lawful data use, accurate eligibility checks, notice, a real way to decline and timely correction. Registration must translate into a usable voting record, including a required signature. This proposal does not change today's registration deadlines or make a person eligible merely because they receive Medicaid.
