# CMS-IMP-02: Oregon contract evidence and incremental-cost follow-up

October 23, 2026 | Research follow-up for Espen Swanson

## Finding

The public record supports a substantially better account of existing Oregon resources than the initial pilot could provide. Two executed contracts are now located: the main ONE system support contract, **PO-DASOBO-00015826**, and Public Consulting Group's training contract, **181633 / PO-10000-00026732**, with signed amendments 4, 5 and 6. The actual current ONE Advance Planning Document, its approved cost-allocation schedules, and a funded enhancement service order relevant to the proposed restoration work remain unlocated in the public sources searched. Their absence from this search does not establish that they do not exist. [R01-R07]

One historical contract increment is reproducible: amendment 4 added **$980,633** of specified SNAP assessment, improvement and training deliverables. Later amendments change its schedule and prices. That is useful evidence of how to reconstruct an increment, but it supplies no Medicaid restoration price or federal matching share. The attempted HLT-1 chain therefore ends at **unpriced incremental requirements and funding**. The accompanying request packet identifies the records needed to finish it; no request has been submitted.

This follow-up supersedes the original pilot's statement that an executed training award was not verified. It also qualifies the June training procurement request with the subsequently executed September amendment. The original workbook's 85 observations remain a dated research snapshot; no new amount has been inserted into it or the project's fiscal inputs.

### The main ONE contract changes the baseline analysis

R01 Section 6.1 caps compensation at **$284,368,568**. Exhibit L separates **$224,368,568** of scheduled application, infrastructure and security deliverables from **$55 million** for enhancement/organizational-change service orders and **$5 million** for cloud-transition service orders. These reconcile exactly to the cap. The $60 million of order ceilings cannot be treated as uncommitted money. Section 6.7 conditions payment on sufficient appropriation or expenditure authority. An OregonBuys field labelled Actual Cost and its master-control spending fields are not invoice or general-ledger evidence. [R01, Sections 6-7, Exhibit L, PDF pp.17-20, 225-227; R10]

The application SOW includes up to **40 Work Items per month**; a Work Item is defined as an enhancement requiring no more than **50 total effort hours** and no JAD session. Both are contractual definitions and limits. There is no utilization series establishing an available balance. Larger enhancements and separately requested change-management work require an authorized, fully executed Service Order with deliverables and prices. The form at PDF pp.58-59 contains blank identifiers and signature fields; it is a template, not a funded order. [R01, SOW 1, Part I and Part II Sections 4.7-4.9]

The rate tables now provide real price inputs, but quantities are missing. For July 2028-June 2029, for example, the onshore mid-level business analyst rate is **$220/hour** and the functional program SME rate **$268/hour**. These are negotiated service-order costing rates under the downloaded contract, not government salary rates or evidence of a staff assignment. They must be checked against subsequent amendments and actual order terms. [R01, Exhibit L, PDF p.228]

The signature page references CMS approval by letter dated **May 9, 2023**, and FNS approval by letter dated **June 6, 2023**. Neither letter is reproduced there. They are precise retrieval leads, not a verified APD, cost allocation or authority for a future HLT-1 change. [R01, PDF p.45]

## 2. Reconstructable historical increment: PCG SNAP Phase V

This is an observed increment in contracted deliverable prices, in the nominal dollars of the signed 2025 amendment. It is neither an actual-spending series nor a cost estimate for Project 2050. Amendment 4 raises the consideration ceiling from $2,183,174 to $3,163,807: **$980,633 added**. The eleven added Phase V rows independently sum to the same amount. [R03, Sections 2.a and Phase V payment table, PDF pp.1, 15-16]

| Phase V deliverable (shortened descriptions) | Added amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Governance design | $8,015.00 |
| Advisory group charter and responsibilities | $8,015.00 |
| Lead-worker initial assessment | $131,955.50 |
| Lead-worker improvement-project list | $38,425.50 |
| Lead-worker facilitation/reporting | $339,840.56 |
| Lead-worker post-implementation report | $23,605.50 |
| Support-staff initial assessment | $107,465.00 |
| Support-staff improvement-project list | $30,645.00 |
| Support-staff facilitation/reporting | $122,160.56 |
| Support-staff post-implementation report | $16,505.00 |
| Lead/support-worker training | $154,000.38 |
| **Added deliverable-price total** | **$980,633.00** |

The monthly descriptions refer to a reporting cadence. The listed amounts are included once in the contract total; multiplying them by a guessed number of months would destroy the reconciliation. Payment still depends on the contract's acceptance and invoicing provisions.

Amendment 6 removes five of those priced rows totalling **$656,112.00** and adds four replacement rows: interim training $50,000.00; final materials/delivery $104,000.80; on-the-job training roadmap/tracker $302,111.60; transition memo $40,110.50. The replacements sum to **$496,222.90**. Thus:

`$980,633.00 - $656,112.00 + $496,222.90 = $820,743.90` for the revised Phase V schedule.

`$2,183,174.00 + $820,743.90 = $3,003,917.90`, matching R05's revised payment-table total.

The schedule decreases by **$159,889.10** from the prior $3,163,807 total. That amount is a signed price-schedule change, not demonstrated deobligation, returned cash or a reusable Medicaid balance. The unchanged Section 3 ceiling and lower schedule may represent a valid ceiling-versus-priced-deliverables distinction; the accounting treatment requires confirmation. The companion requests ask for that reconciliation. [R05, PDF pp.20-21, 30]

This chain connects an identified organization and vendor to a defined scope change, executed approval and a price delta. It stops before verified budget encumbrance, acceptance, invoice, payment and federal claim. It is specifically **SNAP** work. No Medicaid allocation percentage or FFP rate is assigned. Phase III MAGI/non-MAGI materials in the broader training contract are potential assets to inspect for reuse, but access, currency, adaptation effort and 2029 rights/availability still require evidence.

## 3. Attempted HLT-1 incremental-cost chain

**Proposed estimating unit H01:** one Oregon ONE release that removes the Medicaid work-requirement eligibility workflow after HLT-1, preserves the applicable SNAP rules, updates notices and referrals, and trains staff. This is a scoped research proposal within CMS-IMP-02; it does not assume that reversing a rule is a small Work Item or that a whole subscription can be cancelled.

| Link | Public evidence | Missing input and consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adopted policy and responsible program | HLT-1 restoration; OHA medical policy/OEP eligibility and delivery | Need approved rule/cohort specification and a with/without-policy comparison |
| Existing platform/contract | Executed R01, three service bundles, rates and ordering process | Need current amendment register, backlog/utilization, current base scope and order rights |
| Exact restoration release | No HLT-1 CR, accepted requirements, LOE or order located | Gross incremental vendor work remains unpriced; proposed future policy cannot have an assumed existing order |
| Current-law activity potentially removed | R07 describes MWR-specific subscription and links it to the ONE contract | Need executed quote/order, actual use, committed renewals, notice/cancellation and remaining shared purposes. Its $1.8m/year estimate is not a cancellation saving |
| State time and transition | Existing staff and training functions identified in the pilot | Need occupation/task effort, backfill, testing and training quantities, salary/benefit basis and any wind-down charges |
| APD and assignment to programs | Contract signature page references federal letters; R08 names an old APD | Current operational/development APD and allocation approvals unlocated; federal/state shares remain unpriced |
| Budget and actual cash | Contract payment/appropriation conditions visible | Need fund/appropriation, allotment, encumbrance, invoice/acceptance, payment and claim/draw IDs; no funded order has been verified |
| Federal shared service | Pilot establishes CMS Data Services Hub dependency; CMS-IMP-13 remains the common owner | Need CMS service-owner capacity and marginal charges/partner changes; Oregon's NextGen verification product is a different system and must not be confused with the federal Hub |

The defensible expression is:

`Incremental state resource cost = (supported restoration vendor work + state effort + transition + ongoing work) - (same-scope without-policy resource cost).`

For an authorized separately priced July 2028-June 2029 order, a partial labor calculation could use `220 × incremental BA hours + 268 × incremental functional-SME hours`, plus the other evidenced roles and charges. **Neither quantity is known.** Hours already covered by the base service must be accounted for in the comparison and capacity plan. A negotiated fixed-price order may supersede a labor calculation. No assumed hours are filled in merely to produce a number.

Next, trace the resulting eligible amounts to approved program allocation and matching rates; distinguish the federal contribution from state gross cost; reconcile any HLT administration allowance; and deduct only legally usable existing funding for the same increment. A paid historical SNAP price, a service-order ceiling or the state's aggregate ONE budget cannot replace those steps. The result for H01 is **unpriced**, not zero. The request packet's ER01-ER08 closes each missing link.

### FY2029 consequence

The base ONE contract covers only through **June 30, 2029**. Federal FY2029 continues through September 30, so July-September needs a successor/extension and the next state biennium's authority. R05 ends the PCG work in March 2027. Neither agreement establishes staff or contract continuity for the entire federal year. Obtain existing renewal/successor plans and appropriation/cash forecasts where held, without assuming future plans are adopted.

Separate funding availability, signed commitments and cash. Annual subscription estimates in R07 are not a federal fiscal-year payment schedule. Neither an APD approval nor a signed price establishes the timing of federal draws, state cash needs, federal CMS obligations or CMS-IMP-13 outlays. The finite CAP-7S surge and continuing operations still require their own profiles under the adopted fiscal rules. No cost or saving is exported.

## 5. Search boundary, validation and limitations

The search covered public OregonBuys notices and their anonymous attachment links; exact contract/procurement identifiers; indexed Oregon/legislative and Medicaid/CMS records; APD/IAPD/OAPD/approval searches; and exact-identifier searches for service orders and invoices. Primary PDF attachments were downloaded using the public download links exposed by each page, without a sign-in or privileged system. GovTribe/HigherGov results were used to find primary records, not as price authority.

The web reader failed on several OregonBuys pages/attachments and the large OHA reference PDF; ordinary public downloads succeeded. Local PDF extraction and page renders supplied the cited evidence. Scanned signatures and struck-through/new table rows were checked visually because plain text extraction combines superseded and operative amounts. Broad unrelated search results, Medicaid SPAs, Aging and People with Disabilities documents sharing the acronym APD, other states' APDs and unrelated CIE approvals were not substituted for the ONE APD.

No complete current APD, signed individual Deloitte enhancement Service Order, related funding encumbrance, acceptance/invoice/payment chain, or executed NextGen360 subscription was located. The public main-contract page's August 2026 acknowledgement does not supply the missing change document. No records custodian was contacted, no FOIA/public-records request was filed, no fee or external commitment was authorized, and no response is pending. This is a bounded public search, not an exhaustive inventory of agency holdings.

Validation: the eleven historical items sum to $980,633; the ceiling delta matches; the five removed and four replacement items yield a $159,889.10 decrease; the revised Phase V sum plus the earlier base matches $3,003,917.90; and the ONE scheduled amounts plus order ceilings match $284,368,568. All use exact cents. A calculation record and source hash manifest are included in the archive. Unknown quantities, allocations, cash and offsets are retained as null, not numeric zero.
