# Stage G decision memos: the financing settlement batch

Role: the decision memos of stage G's settlement draft ([`../2-settlement.md`](../2-settlement.md)), one batch, sent together so Espen can decide it in one round. Status: **provisional, drafted on the recommendations, November 20, 2026; revised for the milestone review (#470); none decided.** Espen's stage G method: draft on the recommendations, mark new forks provisional, one decision batch, then revise. Each memo, and each point in G1 B, G3 and G9, is decided on its own (`charter/STANDARDS.md`, "Decision memos"). A decision gets a `DEC-` entry from the lead; stage G's step 7 books it.

**Together, at the recommendations,** the package closes FY2040 and every later year to FY2056 on the general fund (−$3.0B in FY2040, −$3.1B in FY2056).
- Debt is 117.9% of GDP in FY2040, against the baseline's 132.7%.
- On health's take-home test, 76.2% of workers come out ahead on health costs only, and 57.8% with the VAT counted. Of workers below 200% of poverty, 90.4% come out ahead with the VAT counted.
- On D3 every fifth below the top comes out ahead, on average (Q1 +$14,308, Q2 +$12,171, Q3 +$10,440, Q4 +$4,553 a household a year over FY2030-39) and in every year FY2033-39.
- D3's revenue-mix test reads 61% over the decade, 54% in FY2040 and 50% in FY2056.

**Not forks, and not in the batch:** the agent's corrections in `../2-settlement.md` part 2: steps 1-3 (GX narrowed, HCA re-derived, the avoidance allowance by tier) and step 5 (the HI grant's end, confirmed by the milestone review).
