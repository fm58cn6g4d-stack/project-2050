**Together, as booked,** the settlement closes FY2040 and every later year to FY2056 on the general fund (−$6.0B in FY2040, −$0.4B in FY2042, the tightest, −$205.4B in FY2056), with the health contribution's base rate at 3.80% from 2035.
- Debt is 117.8% of GDP in FY2040 and 133.1% in FY2056, against the baseline's 132.7% and 180.2%.
- On health's take-home test, 69.0% of workers come out ahead, both ways.
- On D3 every fifth below the top comes out ahead (Q4 +$5,525 a household a year over FY2030-39). The revenue-mix test reads 65% over the decade.
