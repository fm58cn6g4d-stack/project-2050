# Stage G decision memos: the financing settlement batch

Role: the decision memos of stage G's settlement draft ([`../2-settlement.md`](../2-settlement.md)), one batch, sent together so Espen can decide it in one round. Status: **provisional, drafted on the recommendations, November 20, 2026; none decided.** Espen's stage G method: draft on the recommendations, mark new forks provisional, one decision batch, then revise. Each memo, and each point in G1 B and G9, is decided on its own (`charter/STANDARDS.md`, "Decision memos"). A decision gets a `DEC-` entry from the lead; G7 (the stage G step) books it.

**Together, at the recommendations,** the package closes FY2040 and every later year to FY2056 on the general fund (−$11.4B in FY2040, −$8.7B in FY2056). Debt is 117.7% of GDP in FY2040 against the baseline's 132.7%. 88.7% of workers come out ahead on the take-home test, and on D3 every fifth below the top comes out ahead on average (Q1 +$13,896, Q2 +$11,669, Q3 +$9,935, Q4 +$4,102 a household a year over FY2030-39) and in every year FY2033-39. D3's revenue-mix test passes on its window (57%) but is 50% in FY2040 and 46% in FY2056 (memo G3, point G3.2).

**Not forks, and not in the batch:** the agent's corrections in `../2-settlement.md` part 2, step 2 (GX narrowed, HCA re-derived, the avoidance allowance by tier and the HI grant's end), which G5, the milestone review, checks.
