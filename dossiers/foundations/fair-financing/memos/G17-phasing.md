## The question

The plan's general fund runs $193-277B short a year in FY2036-39, about $1.0T in all. That comes from health's 2035 endpoint, before D2's cap and the defense saving bite (part 4). The rule doesn't test those years, but the hump adds to the debt carried into FY2040, and it is what makes "debt falling from the mid-2030s" costly. Should any of the plan's own programs be phased to shrink it?

| | What | Saved FY2035-39 | Who waits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Nothing phased: borrow through the hump | 0 | no one |
| **B (recommended)** | **Clean-energy credits (CLI-1) held at their FY2037 level in FY2038-39 (PH-3)**; long-term care's phasing (PH-1) named in law as the fallback if the hump runs above its projection | $52B | slower clean-energy deployment in two years (OUT-05) |
| C | B plus long-term care reaching full benefit in FY2039 (PH-1: 40/55/70/85% of its FY2035-38 path) | $314B | older and disabled people needing care: about 60% of the benefit's cost deferred in FY2035, 45% in FY2036 and 15% in FY2038, so families wait one to four years longer (OUT-03, OUT-08) |
| D | C plus housing's stage 2 held at its FY2036 level (PH-2) | $323B | also up to about 200,000 families waiting one to three years (OUT-04) |

## What each does

- **On G15 B (held flat from FY2040):** phasing lowers the debt carried into FY2040, by about $57B with interest under B and about $355B under D (0.1 to 0.65 points of GDP). It barely changes the money needed from FY2040.
- **On "debt falling from the mid-2030s":** D lowers the share needed from 1.54% to 1.35% of GDP.
- **Not proposed:**
  - delaying the 2035 endpoint;
  - phasing the child benefit, child care or pre-K, which are the populist start's delivery;
  - triggers that switch a benefit on only after a fiscal finding.

## Recommendation

**B.** On the recommended debt goal the hump is not tested, so the case for making people wait is weak. Hold the clean-energy credits' late-decade bump. Write long-term care's phasing into law as a fallback that takes effect only if the trustees project the FY2036-39 deficits above the booked path. Don't phase care now.

**What would change this:**
- If you choose a goal that tests the mid-2030s, then **C** or **D**.
- If the clean-energy schedule matters more than $52B, then **A**.
