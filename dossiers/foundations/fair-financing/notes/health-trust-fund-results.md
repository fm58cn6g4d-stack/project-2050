## 2. The rate the fund needs, by how the general fund's share is defined

## 3. The fund's reserve if FY2030-34's surplus stays in it

- Of FY2030-34's surplus, 425.4 is HI's credit of H1a and HLT-6's Part A share, which HI already holds and which folds into the fund in 2035.
- The rest, which reaches the general fund's figures, built over FY2030-34: 391.0. Held as the fund's standing reserve: 250.0 (G6 C's amount). Drawn to lower the general fund's transfer: FY2035 141.0.
- Unified debt is unchanged: the money is the same; only the split between the general fund and the fund moves. The general fund's own FY2030-34 position is 391.0 worse, and its FY2035-39 transfer 141.0 smaller.
- Interest the fund would be credited on its reserve is an intragovernmental payment, netted out of the unified budget; it is not modeled.
- **As booked** (stage G step 11; `settlement.json`'s `health_fund`, `recovery-budget/model.py`'s `health_fund()`): the fund holds 391.0 built over FY2030-34, keeps 250.0 as its standing reserve and draws 141.0; the general fund's transfer to the fund is FY2035 154.1, FY2036 553.8, FY2037 587.2, FY2038 585.6, FY2039 554.9, FY2040 537.8, FY2045 754.0, FY2050 958.8, FY2056 1366.1. HI's line is HI's own account until the fold-in, so it is not one of the fund's sources there.
