**Readings.** C: the plan's own channels alone, no feedback. **A: the rate and output feedback from the FY2029 debt ratio, on the baseline and the plan alike (symmetric; the headline).** B: the feedback from CBO's own baseline debt path, so only the plan's difference from current law gets it (a sensitivity: it credits the plan's whole distance below current law at average coefficients, which likely overstates it; see part 2 of `../4-fiscal-path.md`). **Cases.** Pessimistic: every channel at its low end and, on each reading, whichever feedback end gives the plan the higher FY2056 debt (for the goals, whichever end makes each goal dearest by its own test years); optimistic: the reverse. **Not pre-registered:** the engine, this pairing and the readings' roles; the first results were committed about 6.5 minutes after the registration.
