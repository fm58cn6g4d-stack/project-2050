# Fair financing (stage G)

Role: the home of stage G, the financing settlement (`charter/PLAN.md` section 6, stage G; `DEC-2026-11-14-06`). It balances the plan plan-wide, the one place where that happens (`DEC-2026-11-10-05`): it audits the whole plan's financing after stage F, re-checks what the stage E checkpoint settled, and brings Espen one decision batch. Claude leads it (`DEC-2026-11-11-07`). Status: **stage G closed, November 24, 2026: the settlement is booked (step 7).** The fiscal path review (step 8) followed; its batch 3 was decided November 25 (`DEC-2026-11-25-01` to `-11`), and step 9 ([`7-batch3-revision.md`](7-batch3-revision.md)) revises for it, with batch 4 (G23-G25) provisional. The booked choices are `models/data/inputs/settlement.json`; the text is `2-settlement.md`.

## Settled before stage G (not reopened here)

- Health comes first on the plan's revenue (`DEC-2026-11-15-04`), and health's share comes from the top-end and corporate rows first (`-20`).
- The fiscal rule is report, don't gate (`DEC-2026-11-10-05`).
- Every booked flagship design stands as decided.
- The stage E checkpoint's health financing (`DEC-2026-11-15-01` to `-29`, `DEC-2026-11-16-01` to `-03`) is the record in [`../checkpoint-financing.md`](../checkpoint-financing.md). Stage G re-audits it.
