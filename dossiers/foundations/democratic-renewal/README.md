# Foundation: democratic renewal

Role: the democratic renewal foundation (`charter/PLAN.md` 3.1): "a democracy and a government that work for the 21st century". Status: **draft, stage F, step 4 (sections 1 to 4)**. Sections 1 and 2 are research only. Section 3's thirteen memos and 50 yes-or-no points are **decided** (`DEC-2026-11-17-40` to `-99`, November 17, 2026), each on its own: as recommended except memo 9 part 2, changed; memo 9 part 1 and the eight Discuss points were settled on the follow-up note (`-97`, `-98`), its other recommendations became directions for section 4 and memo 13 takes the staged path (`-99`), and several answers carry Espen's terms (`3-design.md`, "Decided"; questions answered in `notes/espen-questions-2026-11-17.md`). Level: the decided items are at **L2** in `agenda/agenda.csv`, with DEM-17 moved to L2 by section 4 and two rows added for decided items that had none (CAP-19, shared rule 5; CRT-20, civil legal aid); directions (DEM-3, DEM-4, DEM-9, CAP-10) and the pointers stay at **L1**. Section 4, the specification, is drafted (137 provisions in `provisions.json`; Espen's go, November 17). L3 follows sections 5 to 9 and the milestone review. Nothing is booked; section 4 reports four unbooked lines for stage G (the immigration court's structure, the library line, statute tracking and legal aid).

**What it covers** (`charter/PLAN.md` 3.1, exactly): "The voting floor, money in politics, ethics, courts, participation, long-run institutional reform, and a government that delivers: agency capacity, payment systems, and remedies when delivery fails. Each flagship's delivery section states its own needs; this foundation owns the cross-cutting state capacity." Stage F sizes that capacity "to the specified flagships" (`charter/PLAN.md` 6, stage F).
