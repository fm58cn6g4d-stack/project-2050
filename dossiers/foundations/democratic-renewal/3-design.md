Status: **decided** (`DEC-2026-11-17-40` to `-94`, Question 0d, November 17, 2026; memo 9 part 1 and the eight provisional points settled, and the follow-up note's recommendations adopted, in `-97` to `-99`), each memo and point on its own, as recommended except where "Decided" below says otherwise. Every one of the 118 rows homed here (`agenda/agenda.csv`, `home_dossier` = `foundations/democratic-renewal`) is either in a memo or in the yes-or-no list (the table "Where each row is decided"). The decided rows move to L2 in `agenda/agenda.csv`; directions stay at L1. This section books nothing and changes no model input.

## How to read this section

- **Thirteen memos and 50 yes-or-no points, sent as one batch** so Espen can answer in one round; each is still decided on its own (`charter/STANDARDS.md`, "Decision memos").
- **Legacy designs are options** (`DEC-2026-10-30-02`, `-03`): each legacy chosen design appears as an option to keep, change or drop, with its file and line, and each memo's "Inherited assumptions, traced" table says whether Espen decided it. Legacy figures are evidence, not defaults (`DEC-2026-11-13-15`); those a memo relies on are in the register as grade D entries (EV-DEM-328 to 332).
- **Costs are reported, not gated** (`DEC-2026-11-10-05`): each option's cost is given against outside scores where any exist; how it is paid for is stage G's.
- **Settled already, not reopened:** D2, the Senate rules question, is a constraint (the scenario's 2027-28 questions); CRT-5's requirements come from the immigration flagship (`DEC-2026-11-12-07`); SAF-8 and LAB-9 are homed elsewhere; executive power and amendments are wave three; CRT-16's trigger was removed on October 26.
