## The question

The legacy record holds a package Espen set on October 19: DEM-3, DEM-4 and DEM-9 "are one package, dealt with together in the second trifecta: run on it in 2032, enact in 2033, one-off 2034 redraw" (`legacy/domains/domain-democracy.md`, line 16). Under `DEC-2026-10-30-02` it is a working choice. Keep it, change it, or decide its parameters now?

## What the evidence says

- **House size:** the cube-root rule gives about 692 members for the 2020 count (a cross-spectrum reform group) [EV-DEM-249]; a peer-reviewed reanalysis finds the rule's fit forced, with an exponent nearer 0.45, and no power law precise enough for policy [EV-DEM-250]. The House has had 435 members since 1913 [EV-DEM-074]; districts average 761,169 people [EV-DEM-075].
- **Ranked-choice voting:** ballot exhaustion left winners short of a majority of all ballots in four large local elections [EV-DEM-251]; theoretical flaws rarely occur otherwise [EV-DEM-252]; nineteen states had banned it by March 2026 [EV-DEM-255].
- **Proportional representation:** New Zealand's 1996 switch raised women from 21% to 29% and Māori members from 7% to 13% of the House [EV-DEM-257]; voters kept it in 2011, 57.77% to 42.23% [EV-DEM-256].

## The options

**A. Keep the package as a direction; decide its parameters in 2031 (recommended).** House size (DEM-3A's cube-root rule, 3B's Wyoming rule or 3C's fixed increase), district magnitude (DEM-4A's five or 4B's three) and the fallback if DEM-9's amendment stalls (DEM-4C, a statutory presidential ranked-choice vote, or 4D, hold STV until ratified) go to a 2031 review, after the 2030 census and the Section 2 rerun the legacy file requires (line 158).
- *For:* the inputs that decide the parameters (the census, *Callais*'s effect on minority representation, the 2030 maps) do not exist yet; the package keeps STV from shipping with a plurality presidency (line 154).
- *Against:* the legacy file wants the fallback "chosen before 2032" (line 159); A meets that only if the 2031 review happens.

**B. Decide the parameters now** (the legacy chosen options: 692 seats, five-member districts; and pick 4C or 4D).
- *For:* the 2032 campaign has a fixed plank.
- *Against:* decides on a yardstick the evidence contests [EV-DEM-250] and before the Section 2 rerun.

**C. Unbundle: House enlargement alone, earlier.**
- *For:* enlargement is the most legible piece and needs no amendment.
- *Against:* breaks Espen's October 19 package; a larger House drawn in single-member districts is a different reform.

**D. Drop the package.** *Against:* abandons the plan's only answer to winner-take-all representation; no evidence argues for dropping it.
