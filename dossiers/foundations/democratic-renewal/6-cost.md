# Democratic renewal: cost

Role: section 6 of the democratic renewal foundation. It prices every provision of `4-specification.md` and `5-delivery.md` on federal fiscal years against the January 2029 law, sets each beside what the budget model books, and reports the fiscal-rule figures to the financing settlement (`charter/STANDARDS.md`, "Fiscal rule in dossiers: report, don't gate"; `DEC-2026-11-10-05`). Status: **booked** (stage F, booking, tier 3), on `DEC-2026-11-17-40` to `-99` and `DEC-2026-11-18-11` to `-32`, after the milestone review's fixes and the L3 re-check (`charter/STANDARDS.md`, "Milestone review"). Parts 1 to 8 are the pricing as drafted, with the numbers restated where the booking moved them; **part 9 says what the booking changed** and part 7 how each decided item was booked. The booked rows are in `models/data/inputs/` and are written by `models/tools/democratic_renewal_booking.py`; `models/tools/test_fiscal_democratic_renewal.py` holds them equal to it. Every figure is project arithmetic or a restated outside score, not an official score.

## Counting rules

- **Conventions.** Federal cost, nominal $ billion by federal fiscal year (FY y runs from October of y−1), a cost positive, **on the general fund** unless a line says otherwise, against the January 2029 law.
- **The booked basis.** The budget model's central case, `mixed`, read from its audit lines (`legacy/sources/recovery-budget-audit.csv`) on main after #402, not rebuilt. CAP-7S and CAP-7 are booked at Espen's October 26 sizes through the model's line "Smaller capacity and AI authorizations", which scales the ledger rows ($560 billion and $456 billion) to $98.0 billion and $279.2 billion (`models/tools/budget/note-cap7-sizes.md`). This section quotes the scaled figures as the booked ones. The booking (part 9) adds four rows to that basis and changes none of those figures.
- **The plan-wide gap** is the general-fund primary balance of the whole plan in FY2040, case `mixed`: **$615.6 billion short** (1.14% of GDP; `models/data/recovery-budget-paths.csv`, `agenda_gf_primary_bn`, after this booking). It was $614.0 billion before this booking (after work's, #459) and $602.7 billion before that (after #402; the board rounded it to $603 billion). Each share below is of the $615.6 billion, on the same basis (additive on the primary balance before interest). It moves whenever another dossier is booked.
- **The scenario and the booking.** `models/bookings/democratic_renewal_cost.py` lays the booked rows and the priced lines on fiscal years, and `models/tools/democratic_renewal_booking.py` turns the lines section 6 books into the input rows, so this section and the budget model read the same numbers (run the cost tool with no flag for the tables, `--check` for its self-checks). The cost tool reads the audit lines and writes nothing.
- **The ledger rounds each spending row to $1 billion** (`ledger.json`), so a booked row can differ from its priced line by up to $0.5 billion: the library line is booked at $8.0 billion for $7.8 billion priced, legal aid $5.0 billion for $4.6 billion, and D4's outlay row −$17.0 billion for −$16.6 billion. The tables say which they quote.
- **Spending, revenue and contingent money stay apart** (`dossiers/TEMPLATE.md`, "One basis for each fiscal figure", "Say what a model figure leaves out"). Revenue effects (the 1043 deferral, the IRS's enforcement yield) have their own lines. A reserve released only when its gates clear is shown from zero to its cap, never added to firm money. An authorization subject to appropriation is labeled so.
- **Ranges** run each line's low and high inputs together: a bound, not a probability interval.
- **What each input is** (commitment, working assumption, open question, study in implementation) is in part 10.

## 2. The match (INT-5.01)

- **The outside score.** CBO estimated in March 2021 that H.R. 1's match, for House candidates only, up to $6 for every $1 from donors giving $200 or less, would cost about **$620 million for the 2028 election and about $850 million for the 2030 election**, about $1.6 billion over 2021-2031 (CBO, 2021) [EV-DEM-355]. That bill's match was not limited to in-state gifts.
- **Scaling it to this design** (project arithmetic, working assumption):
  - *Senate candidates added:* the cost rises by half to the whole of the House cost (judgment; Senate races raise less in small gifts per candidate but are statewide);
  - *in-state gifts only:* a share of 0.6 to 0.9 of small gifts is matched (judgment; out-of-state small giving is concentrated in a few national races);
  - so the 2030-cycle equivalent is about **$0.8-1.5 billion a cycle**, growing about 10% a cycle as participation and the ceilings rise.
  - *First matched cycle 2032:* the act is enacted by spring 2030 at the latest, and the FEC needs the fund, the qualifying rules and the payment system in place first, as CBO's own estimate assumed some years' lead.
- **Over FY2030-39:** four cycles (2032 to 2038), about **$6 billion (range $4-8 billion)**; with ceilings and participation at the high end, up to about $12 billion. **FY2040:** about $0.6-1.2 billion a year, averaged over the 2040 cycle.

The central estimate fits inside it with room for DEM-2.05's commission grants.
