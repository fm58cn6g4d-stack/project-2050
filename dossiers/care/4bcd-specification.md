# Care you can count on, parts B-D (long-term care, child care and pre-K, the care workforce): specification

Role: section 4 of the care dossier for parts B, C and D. It writes the decided designs as provisions: what each does, its parameters, who is covered and who pays, the vehicle, the agency, how it changes current law, and the evidence it rests on. Provision IDs and their fields are in `provisions.json`, beside health's. Status: draft (stage D, board row D-9, PR 1), on Espen's decisions `DEC-2026-11-11-02` (part B), `-03` (part C), `-04` (part D), `-05` (the cost direction) and `-06` (the cost options and states' shares). Health's sections 4-9 (`4-specification.md` and on) are decided and at L3; this file changes none of health's commitments, and amends health's HLT-7.07 only as part B decided. Costs are in `6bcd-cost.md`; each provision's `cost_source` points there. The layout (one set of files for parts B-D) is recorded in `README.md`.

## How to read this section

- **Status of each provision.** *Decided* means Espen decided it: a memo option, a yes-or-no point or a later decision, cited. *A working choice* is a parameter this section sets so the draft is complete; it rests on judgment and can change without reopening a decision. *Open* marks a question left open under "What needs an answer now" (`DEC-2026-11-10-07`), with its alternatives. No provision waits on Espen.
- **Commitments and working assumptions.** This section commits to who is covered, the benefit, the threshold, the rate rules, the dates, the protections and the states' shares as decided. The tier amounts, the room-and-board line, the protected allowance, the salary scale's steps and the take-up behind every cost are working assumptions, with ranges in section 6.
- **Dates.** The plan begins in January 2029 (`DEC-2026-10-29-01`). Calendar dates are when people are covered; fiscal years are for money; child care and pre-K run on school years, the first being 2030-31. Two items reach before 2029 and are labeled: Money Follows the Person (a 2027-28 extender if one moves) and preparation.
- **Evidence.** The last column lists the evidence IDs a provision rests on. Where a parameter is a working choice with no entry, the column says so. "Project arithmetic" is `tools/care_repricing.py` and `tools/care_bcd_path.py`.
- **Agenda homes.** HLT-4 carries Medicaid's long-term care until 2035 (the bridge and its protections). HLT-9 carries the home and community care benefit and the care part of institutional care in the single plan. HLT-10 carries the care wage floor and the workforce points; HLT-11 the nursing home staffing standard. FAM-2 carries child care and pre-K; FAM-2G early educators' pay.

**Why this split.**
- *Reconciliation carries money, not rules without a budget effect* (the Byrd rule). The bridge's points, the wage floor (a condition of payment that changes federal spending), the resource limits and child care's mandatory money all change outlays. Build Back Better carried a whole child care and pre-K program in a reconciliation bill [EV-CAR-587], so the program's rules that set who is paid and how much can ride with its money.
- *The home benefit is a title of the Medicare for All Act*, decided with memo 6 B (`memos/6-home-care-guarantee.md`), because it is a benefit of the single plan and starts on the plan's endpoint.
- *The staffing standard goes in the regular-order Act*, not R1 (a working choice; memo 16 leaves it to this section). A minimum staffing rule's budget effect is arguably incidental to its policy, which the Byrd rule strikes. Its money still rides the rates the Act sets.
- *If the rules reform fails*, health's fallback applies (`5-delivery.md`, part 3): the plan's money moves to a later reconciliation act. For parts B-D that means the benefit's spending (HLT-9's budgets and the institutional care part) can ride that act, while the staffing standard, the assessment's rules and the family-caregiver decision point wait for 60 votes. Section 8 names it.
- *If R2 slips past January 2030*, child care's dates run from enactment with a latest date (the first school year that starts at least nine months after enactment), as housing's R2 provisions do (`dossiers/home/4-specification.md`, "Dates").

## Where particular groups fall

Groups the provisions do not name, each placed by a working choice that follows from the decisions (the milestone review's M-3); none needs a new decision:
- **The territories.** Residents are entitled to the benefit from 2035 under HLT-7.01 and HLT-7.28. The bridge and the 2030-34 floor are paid outside the capped allotments (HLT-4.01, HLT-10.01), child care and pre-K run on the states' terms (FAM-2.07, 2.08), and the territories' long-term care payment follows HLT-7.28 (HLT-9.09).
- **Veterans.** The VA's home care, community nursing homes, state veterans homes and caregiver stipend continue (HLT-7.24). For a veteran eligible for both, the VA pays first for care it provides or purchases, and the plan's benefit covers needs the VA does not; the tier budget is reduced by VA-paid hours of the same service, so one need is not paid twice. A VA caregiver stipend and paid family care under HLT-9.05 may both be drawn only for different hours (working choice).
- **Children in foster and kinship care.** Covered under FAM-2.06's every-resident rule. The child alone counts as the family for the income determination, as CCDF allows today, so a foster or kinship child's care is free whatever the caregivers' income.
- **Medicare home health and skilled nursing stays.** Post-hospital home health episodes and skilled nursing stays stay plan medical care (HLT-7.05, HLT-9.08). During an episode the tier budget continues for long-term services the episode does not cover; the same hours are not billed twice, and no reassessment is needed (HLT-9.06).
- **People in prison or jail.** As in health's placement (`4-specification.md`, "Where particular groups fall"): the custodial authority pays for long-term care inside its facilities; the plan covers people from the day they are released, and the benefit's assessment can be done before release so help starts on that day.

- **Three daily activities, not two** (`DEC-2026-11-11-02`), with the hold-harmless (HLT-9.01, 9.02), and a state option below the line (HLT-4.08).
