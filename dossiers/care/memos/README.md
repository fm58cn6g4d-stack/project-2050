# Care decision memos (health part): the batch

Role: the one set of health decisions for Espen, sent together so they could be decided in one round (`../3-design.md`). Status: decided. Espen answered the batch on November 10, 2026 (`DEC-2026-11-10-09`). Two questions were reopened and answered after the comparison with S. 1506 (`../research-s1506-comparison.md`; `DEC-2026-11-10-11`). Two follow-ups were then decided: private coverage and ownership (`DEC-2026-11-10-12`) and the hospital transition fund (`DEC-2026-11-10-13`).

| Memo | Question | Decided |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1](1-architecture.md) | Single-payer, a public option or a staged path | B, a staged path to single-payer with the endpoint in the 2029 law; the endpoint is January 1, 2035 (`-09`, `-11`) |
| [2](2-transition.md) | In what order people move, and the protections | By payer, children first: wave 1 in 2031; Medicaid and employer groups together, required, in 2033-35; everyone by 2035; all five protections (`-09`, `-11`) |
| [3](3-financing-interface.md) | How money flows into the health system | B: keep the public streams; states' maintenance of effort; an employer contribution until the settlement's payroll contribution; the end of the tax exclusion as a named stream, counted only on premiums that become wages (`-09`, `-11`) |
| [4](4-benefits.md) | What the plan covers and what people pay | C: comprehensive benefits, small capped copays above 200% of poverty; abortion and reproductive care covered, and the Hyde Amendment ended in general; long-term care in part B (`-09`, `-11`) |
| [5](5-provider-payment.md) | What the plan pays providers | B: a five-year glide to about 1.15 times Medicare, global budgets for rural and safety-net hospitals, the Medicare Advantage fix (`-09`); a capped hospital transition fund (`-13`); ownership kept with guardrails, no duplicative coverage at the endpoint (`-12`) |

`../3-design.md` also has seven yes-or-no points, all decided yes, with point 6 as option (b) (`-09`). The VA, TRICARE and the Indian Health Service continue alongside the plan (`-11`). The specification is `../4-specification.md`.
