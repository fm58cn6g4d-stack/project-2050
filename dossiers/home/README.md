# Dossier: A home you can keep (flagship 3)

Role: the housing flagship (`charter/PLAN.md` 3.1). Status: in progress, stage C. Level: the housing items Espen decided in section 3 (`DEC-2026-11-09-01` to `-06`, the tax credits in `DEC-2026-11-09-08`, and the later directions in `-09` to `-11`) are at L2 in `agenda/agenda.csv`; the rest stay at L1 (`DEC-2026-10-30-03`, PR #75); their designs are carried over from `legacy/domains/domain-housing.md`. The target is L3, with priority pieces at L4.

**What it brings together:**
- rental help as a guarantee;
- building and repair;
- zoning incentives.

**Agenda items:** HOU-1 to HOU-9 and their options (`agenda/agenda.csv`, `home_dossier` = `home`).

**Current law:** the ROAD to Housing Act of July 11, 2026 is the baseline (`road-act.md`).

**Outcomes it serves:**
- OUT-04, a home people can keep;
- OUT-02, planning beyond the next paycheck;
- OUT-01, child poverty.

Sections 4-7 carry them. Two small parameters are still proposed: each state's share of the direct production count (HOU-3C.04), and the low-income credit's high-cost boost for rural areas (HOU-2D.01; the tribal boost is decided).
2. **Optional uploads for section 2.** HUD's Family Options Study reports and *A Picture of Subsidized Households*, and CBO's analysis of the 2023 immigration surge (publication 61464), would replace secondary summaries. The list is at the end of `2-what-works.md`.

4. **Section 5's delivery questions** are decided (`DEC-2026-11-09-10`): R1 readiness money, tribal set-asides, the wider services backstop through 2035, fair housing testing money, deemed approval and private rights of action with fees.
5. **Housing cost (section 6).** Questions 1 and 5 are decided (`DEC-2026-11-09-09`). Still open: stage 3's age and severe-problems tests, the emergency fund's size, the zoning reward after 2039, stage 2's take-up assumption and the fee rate (`6-cost.md`, "Questions for Espen").

Still open: a stated route for single adults and families just above the line; a target for the social housing pilot; and whether voucher rent should ignore the value of free child care (`7-validation.md`, "Questions for Espen").
