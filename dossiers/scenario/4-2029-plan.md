## The stage order in `charter/PLAN.md`

The plan's order is reset (A), post-election update (B), housing and transportation (C), care and the secure floor (D), climate and immigration (E), work and democratic renewal (F), fair financing (G), toolkit (H), then waves two and three. The results suggest four possible changes, none made here.

1. **A short stage B follow-up after certification.** Counts, the Alaska ranked-choice result, Louisiana's runoffs and the first validated analyses arrive between November 18 and mid-December. A light recheck of `1-results.md` then, and of `2-analysis.md` after the validated studies in 2027, keeps the scenario current at little cost (framework question 12).
2. **Parts of democratic renewal earlier.** Protecting the 2028 count, the *Callais* response in DEM-1, and the oversight evidence on agency damage are all live in 2027-28. Stage F sits after D and E. Option: pull the DEM-1 and election-administration pieces forward into a narrow step during stage D, leaving the rest of F in place.
3. **Care first is reinforced.** Health care is Democrats' largest issue lead (50-30), the ACA credits lapsed, 2027 benchmark premiums are up about 13% [SRC-0538], and Medicaid work requirements start January 1, 2027. Stage D already comes next after housing; no change, but its design work would inform any 2027 health bill.
4. **Immigration's timing.** The plan specifies immigration "well before the 2028 cycle" (stage E). Republicans kept a narrow lead on immigration (43-41) while 58% said enforcement had gone too far, and Trump has said he would "consider" an immigration deal [SRC-0521]. Option: keep stage E's place, or start the immigration problem section earlier so the project has a position before any 2027 negotiation.

Housing (stage C) is unaffected: the memos remain Espen's, and nothing in the results changes them.
