# The pre-build pipeline: what a 2027-28 House could pass for each flagship

Role: for each flagship and the democratic-renewal foundation, what a House-passed 2027-28 bill would contain, its vehicle, and whether it would lock in a weaker design than the dossier is likely to choose. It expands section 1 of `compounding-2029-2050.md`. Status: draft (stage B, step 3, round 2), November 8, 2026. **Options, not decisions.** No flagship design is decided: legacy designs are working choices (`DEC-2026-10-30-02`) at L1 (`DEC-2026-10-30-03`). Housing memos 1B, 2B (with a stage for disabled renters) and HOU-7B are working directions, not recorded decisions. "Likely dossier choice" is this project's reading of the legacy working design, which the dossier may change.

## In brief

- **Four bills could be full text now with little lock-in risk, because they restore prior law or carry text from earlier Congresses:** HLT-1's ACA and Medicaid restoration, CLI-1's clean-energy credits, DEM-1's voting package and LAB-1's PRO Act. Each would be veto bait or filibustered, but would hand the 2029 Congress committee records and CBO scores.
- **The lock-in danger is in the deals, not in the House bills.** The bills a House passes alone fix nothing in law. The designs Trump might sign do: a Vance child tax credit, drug pricing on Trump's design, a $15 minimum wage, a Dreamers-for-enforcement trade, a Congress-only stock ban. Each would set the 2029 starting point (`compounding-2029-2050.md`, section 2).
- **Housing is the best-prepared flagship for 2027-28 appropriations.** Voucher, HOME and homelessness increases fit every working choice (memos 1B and 2B, HOU-7B), and the ROAD Act's programs need money to start.
- **Hold three:** immigration (IMM-1, stage E not started), Social Security (D8, its own 2030 bill), and the carbon fee (D7, a 2028 liability for no 2027-28 gain).

## 1. The pipeline

Modes as in `compounding-2029-2050.md`: *full text* (a bill a 2029 Congress could pass as is), *direction* (goal, population and scale, parameters left to the dossier), *hold*. Vehicles: **R27** and **R28**, reconciliation under FY2027 and FY2028 resolutions (veto bait unless narrowed; `2027-28-playbook.md`, section 2a); **AP**, appropriations; **MP**, a must-pass bill; **SA**, a standalone House bill with a Senate cloture vote.
