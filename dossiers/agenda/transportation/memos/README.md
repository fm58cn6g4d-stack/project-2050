# Transportation decision memos: the batch

Role: the one set of transportation decisions for Espen, sent together so they can be decided in one round (`../3-design.md`). Status: decided. Espen answered "yes to all as recommended" on November 10, 2026: memos 1-6 and the eight points in `../3-design.md` (`DEC-2026-11-10-08`), with the transportation outcome added as OUT-12.

| Memo | Question | Decided (as recommended) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1](1-who-pays-for-roads.md) | How fuel taxes and electric cars pay for road repair | A: index, ten 3-cent steps, a per-mile charge on electric cars at a 40-mpg rate, a fossil premium in FY2040-44 (as chosen) |
| [2](2-trucks-rail-air.md) | What heavy trucks, railroads and flyers pay | A: a weight-distance charge for heavy trucks, rail diesel by railroad class, a $16 aviation surcharge (as chosen) |
| [3](3-size-and-split.md) | How large, and how the money is split | A: $936.57B, split 35 / 50 / 15 then 45 / 40 / 15; the old ceiling kept only as a comparison |
| [4](4-transit-operating-help.md) | Federal help to run transit | D: $35B, keeping maintenance of effort, the service floor and the safety rule; two accounting tests become published reports |
| [5](5-conditions-on-states.md) | How hard to push states toward repair | B: a lower federal share for new lanes; condition trends and lifecycle analyses published, with a review |
| [6](6-passenger-rail.md) | How much passenger rail, and how money waits for readiness | A: $254.03B with the gates and caps (as chosen) |

Also in `../3-design.md`, "Recommended without a memo": eight yes-or-no points, including what to do if no multi-year highway law passes by the end of 2028 (point 1) and whether the charter gets a transportation outcome (point 7, a charter change in its own PR).
