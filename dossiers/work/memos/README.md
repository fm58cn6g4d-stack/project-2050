# Work: decision memos

Role: index of the work flagship's decision memos, the batch Espen decides in one round (`dossiers/TEMPLATE.md`, "Decision memos in one batch"). Status: **decided** November 17, 2026 (`DEC-2026-11-17-01` to `-36`; memos `-01` to `-13`, with follow-ups `-32` to `-35`), each memo and point on its own. Three answers differ from the recommendations: memo 1 part 2 **C**, memo 3 **D** and memo 6 part 3 **C**. Memo 9 is settled at about 200,000 positions (`-32`). Memo 8 part 2 is decided for now on the clarified part d of `../notes/espen-questions-2026-11-17.md` (`-95`), with a direction to explore several options for the future labor economy (`-96`). **Memo 10**, written with section 4 as `-96` directs, is **open, for Espen**, and drafted on its recommendation as LAB-19 (provisional); section 4 (`../4-specification.md`) carries the decided design of memos 1-9. The table below shows the recommendations as sent; the decided options are in each memo's status line. Each memo is decided on its own, and none is adopted by silence. Section 3 (`../3-design.md`) has the forks at a glance, the cost benchmarks, the bill-by-bill table, the inherited assumptions traced, the answers to section 2's eleven questions and sixteen yes-or-no points.

Every memo opens with a **decision line** in the same form: the recommended option, its cost over FY2030-39 and in FY2040, and the evidence it rests on. Costs are against the January 2029 law, on the general fund unless marked; a minus sign is a saving. None is booked in the budget model; the financing is stage G's (`DEC-2026-11-10-05`).
