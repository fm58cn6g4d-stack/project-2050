# PROJECT 2029: MASTER EXECUTIVE ACTION INVENTORY
## Day 1 Through Day 30 — Consolidated Across All Policy Domains
### Version 1.0 — March 2026 | Operational Planning Document

---

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

This document consolidates every executive action proposed across all Project 2029 chapters into a single coordinated inventory. It:

- **Eliminates redundancy** between chapters that would otherwise send conflicting direction to the same agencies
- **Sequences for political visibility** — not all EOs are equal; some are ceremony, some are substance, some are both
- **Assigns agency leads** so transition planning has a clear drafting and review chain
- **Flags companion bills** so every EO has a legislative counterpart in preparation
- **Provides a Day-by-Day rhythm** for the first month that communicates competence, urgency, and order of priorities

All EOs should be pre-drafted and reviewed by the DOJ Office of Legal Counsel before Inauguration Day. No action on this list should be signed without OLC sign-off. Where legal authority is contested, the OLC review should identify the strongest statutory and constitutional basis and note the litigation risk.

## COORDINATION AND CONFLICT-CHECK NOTES

### Overlapping Agency Authorities

Several EOs in this inventory involve the same agencies and must be coordinated:

- **HHS/CMS**: Receives direction from EOs 1, 6-13, 41-43, 44-48, 69, 77. CMS is the most operationally burdened agency on Day 1. A dedicated CMS transition team with a specific Day 1-30 implementation calendar is essential.
- **DHS**: EOs 5, 22-32, 52, 56-57, 72. Immigration enforcement direction must be internally consistent; all DHS EOs should be reviewed together by DHS General Counsel for operational coherence before signing.
- **DOJ**: EOs 15-21, 56, 70, 78. DOJ actions must be coordinated: the Government Integrity Unit (EO-21) and the DOGE Accountability Office (EO-70) must have separate chains of command to prevent any appearance of political direction.
- **State/USAID**: EOs 38-40, 59, 73-74. Consolidate international briefings; State and USAID restored operations should be announced in a single international engagement event.

### The "Legal Authority is Contested" Flag

The following EOs involve legal authority that will face immediate court challenge. OLC review must specifically address each:

- **EO-5** (DACA restoration): SCOTUS has previously upheld EO rescission; new EO must be on even stronger administrative grounds than the Obama-era DACA — document the reliance interests thoroughly
- **EO-28** (Denaturalization halt): Novel legal territory; needs strong 5th Amendment due process grounding
- **EO-56** (Third-country removal): Will be litigated immediately; prepare emergency injunction application in parallel
- **EO-68** (Offshore drilling moratorium): Mineral Leasing Act authority; document carefully

### EOs That Are Also Legislative Priorities

Every EO in this inventory should have a companion bill introduced in Congress within the first 30 days. The following are highest legislative priority because they address the most contestable executive authorities:

1. Medicaid work requirement pause → Healthcare Restoration Act (reconciliation)
2. Schedule F rescission → Civil Service Restoration and Protection Act
3. IG reinstatement → IG Restoration and Independence Act
4. DACA restoration → DREAM Act / Comprehensive Immigration Reform
5. Enforcement priorities → Enforcement Standards statutory codification
6. Family separation ban → Family Protection Act
