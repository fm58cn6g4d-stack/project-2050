# Stage H: the front door's outline, "A day in 2050"

Role: the first stage H deliverable, a scene-by-scene outline of the rebuilt front door (`charter/PLAN.md` 3.5; `1-audit.md`, part 2.3), for Espen to react to before the content step (H8) and the Figma design step (H9). Status: **draft outline, November 25, 2026. Not public copy and not published.** It assumes memo H4's option A (one illustrated day across every flagship and foundation); if Espen picks another option it shrinks accordingly. Every scene draws only on its dossier's reviewed public case (section 9) and its composite stories; H8 rewrites the words and traces every figure through a claim register before anything goes public.

## The idea

One ordinary day in one town in 2050, following a few neighbors from early morning to night. Nothing in it is a forecast: each scene is labeled as an imagined scene, and each shows one thing that is different because of a law the plan would pass from 2029, with the year that law starts. A reader can stop at the pictures, or open any scene to see what made the difference, when it begins, the honest limits and the hard questions, and from there the full brief and the model behind it.

The day ends with the money: what it costs, who pays and who comes out ahead.

## What each scene opens to

Each scene is a picture and two or three sentences. Opening it shows, in this order:
1. **What changed**, in plain words, with the provision's plain name.
2. **When it begins.**
3. **What it costs**, as the dossier states it, with "about" and "on our estimates".
4. **The honest limit** (the column above) and one or two hard questions with their answers, from the section 9.
5. **Go deeper:** the flagship's brief (its section 9), the dossier, and the model it rests on, with that model's card and validation table (H8's per-model docs pages).
