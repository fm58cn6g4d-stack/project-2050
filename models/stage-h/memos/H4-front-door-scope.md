# Memo H4: The front door's scope

Role: decision memo for Espen, stage H step 1 (`../1-audit.md`, part 2). Status: **provisional, drafted November 25, 2026. Not decided.** Decided on its own, not as a package.

## The question

PLAN 3.5 asks for the illustrated "day in 2050" front door, rebuilt on the specified flagships, and a research brief and organizing kit for each flagship. The October front door is out of date throughout (part 2.2) and is rebuilt either way. How much should stage H build?

## The options

**A. One illustrated day across all eight flagships and foundations, plus a toolkit page (recommended).**
- Eight scenes (the secure floor, care, housing, clean power, work, immigration, democratic renewal, fair financing; transportation as a ninth), each with what made the difference, the law behind it and when it starts, hard questions, and links to the dossier's public case (section 9) and the model it rests on. A toolkit page for professionals replaces the analysis companion. The October story essays are retired or rewritten into the scenes.
- *For:* delivers PLAN's front door and the toolkit's public face in stage H's three front door steps. The section 9s are already the briefs in substance.
- *Against:* no separate organizing kits yet.

**B. A, plus a brief and an organizing kit per flagship.**
- Each flagship gets a designed brief and a kit (talking points, who gains, stories, hard questions).
- *For:* everything PLAN 3.5 lists. *Against:* about eight more sessions; better done once the 2028 campaign's needs are clearer.

**C. A smaller page now; the full day after the fiscal path settles.**
- A one-page summary of the flagships now; the illustrated day once G15 and G19 are confirmed.
- *For:* no risk of showing money figures that change. *Against:* the money section is only one scene, and A already writes it last; C delays the illustrated vision PLAN asks for.

## Recommendation

**A.** Organizing kits follow as their own steps when Espen wants them.

## Consequences

- **A:** H8 to H10 as planned (three sessions); the Figma file covers the day, the scene page, the money section and the toolkit page.
- **B:** about eight more sessions after H10.
- **C:** H8 to H10 are split, with the illustrated work after the fiscal path revision.
