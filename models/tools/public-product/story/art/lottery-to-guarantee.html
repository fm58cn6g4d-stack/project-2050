Today: a lottery

Rental help is funded as a fixed budget. About one in four eligible households gets it; the rest wait on lists that may never reach them.

Help depends on luck.

First stage: funded expansion

One million new rental-assistance awards, more money for rental help beyond them, and capital to build and repair homes.

More help, still rationed.

Destination: a guarantee

Every household in the defined lowest-income group that applies is served, phased in group by group, with a federal route where a state declines.

Help depends on need.
