Open: HLT-4 + FAM-3 + LAB-3
Unresolved: workforce, access and local availability

Storyboard: select “caring for someone” → compare two authored accounts of dependable and interrupted support → see the policy mechanism and conditions → inspect sources. Turning points navigate chapters; ages and dates do not animate a modeled lifetime. No automatic household dollar balance.

Strength: emotional continuity; exposes interaction between money, time and usable services. Limit: can imply a typical life, obscure institutions and narrow the platform to family benefits. Multiple household forms, disability, migration and nonparent lives need deliberate editorial space. Best as a later series inside the recommended direction.

Storyboard: choose a question → inspect a claim and its evidence → compare only compatible, precomputed cases → download the record and reproduce it. A future comparison selector would enumerate model cases with matched scope; it would not invite arbitrary policy mixing.

Strength: direct, inspectable entry for skeptical readers, journalists and practitioners. Limit: the program can become a collection of caveats and spreadsheets before the reader understands its purpose. Use this structure as the professional layer, with independent entry, beneath the community experience.

Recommendation: use the community as the initial public front door, the casebook as its evidence layer, and life-course stories as later authored features. This pass implements the first journey, not all three interfaces.
