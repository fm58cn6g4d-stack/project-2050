# Project 2050: outcomes record

Role: what the project is trying to change in people's lives, and how it will know. Status: **draft for Espen's approval** (stage A, October 28, 2026). It is the "small outcomes record" the charter asks for (`legacy/governing/project-charter.md`, section on long-run pathways). Each dossier's problem and validation sections cite these IDs.

**How to read it.**
- Each row is an aspiration with the people it must reach, candidate measures and the flagships whose mechanisms are meant to move it.
- Baselines are to be established from the named sources, each as an evidence entry with a date. This draft quotes no numbers, so nothing here outruns the evidence.
- **No numerical targets are set.** Targets, like any new instrument or governing test, are Espen's decisions, made through decision memos as the dossiers mature.
- Aspirations, adopted commitments, observed conditions and conditional model results stay separate.
- A good trend does not show that a policy caused it, and a good average does not show that every community shares the gain.
