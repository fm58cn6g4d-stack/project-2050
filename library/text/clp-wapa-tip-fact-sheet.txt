Empowering American energy
infrastructure in the West

WAPA’s Transmission
Infrastructure Program
The Transmission Infrastructure Program
(TIP) is a strategic federal financing
initiative that leverages Western Area
Power Administration’s (WAPA’s) statutory
borrowing authority and deep expertise in
transmission development to strengthen and
expand the nation’s electric grid. TIP supports
projects that enhance reliability, affordability
and resilience, ensuring that America’s
energy infrastructure meets the demands
of a growing economy and evolving
energy landscape.
TIP manages WAPA’s $3.25 billion revolving
loan program, supporting transmission
projects that facilitate the efficient delivery
of power, enhance grid security and
promote domestic energy development.
Additionally, TIP provides technical and
analytical assistance to eligible partners,
helping advance projects that spur
economic opportunity and modernize
critical infrastructure.

BRIDGING MARKET GAPS TO UNLOCK INVESTMENT
While private investment plays a critical role in energy
infrastructure development, market-driven solutions
alone have not been sufficient to bridge the transmission
gap in the West. Many large-scale transmission projects
face significant hurdles, including long development
timelines, permitting challenges and financing difficulties.
TIP provides a strategic federal financing mechanism that
de-risks projects, enabling private capital to flow more
effectively into the grid expansion needed to sustain
economic growth and energy security.
By leveraging federal loan authority, TIP helps overcome
financial barriers and unlock new or upgraded
transmission projects that are essential to regional and
national energy resilience. These critical projects may not
attract immediate or sufficient private-sector investment
due to perceived risks, costs or long payback periods. This
public-private partnership model ensures that critical
infrastructure is built while maintaining the efficiency and
dynamism of market-driven energy solutions.

STRENGTHENING AMERICA’S ENERGY FUTURE
Originally established to stimulate investment in vital
energy infrastructure during the Great Recession, TIP
continues to support projects that drive economic growth
and national energy security. As electricity demand grows
from data centers, artificial intelligence needs and growing
domestic manufacturing, states are actively developing
diverse energy resources, driving the need for modernized
electricity transmission infrastructure to ensure reliability
and affordability for consumers. TIP plays a pivotal role
providing debt capital to bridge infrastructure gaps, foster
public-private partnerships that accelerate investment
and ensure that the energy grid keeps pace with
economic expansion.
ADVANCING TRANSMISSION TO STRENGTHEN
ECONOMIC GROWTH
Powering economic development
Investment in transmission and energy storage
infrastructure fuels economic growth by creating jobs,
enhancing energy reliability and unlocking new business
opportunities. TIP is uniquely positioned to facilitate these
advancements, supporting projects that enhance grid
efficiency and stability.
Supporting project development & bankability
TIP collaborates with developers to overcome key project
hurdles, from siting and permitting to engineering and
financial structuring – critical steps in securing private
investment. Once a project has successfully navigated
the development phase, it may qualify for TIP financing,
ensuring a streamlined path to implementation.
Harnessing innovation for a resilient grid
With WAPA’s expertise and access to federal financing at
competitive rates, TIP provides a powerful platform to
advance cutting-edge transmission solutions. By working
with qualified developers, TIP ensures that vital projects
move forward, reinforcing energy security, economic
competitiveness and modern infrastructure.

Montana Alberta Tie Ltd
Energized in 2013, this 230-kilovolt transmission project
facilitates the delivery of energy from Montana to
interconnected markets. TIP provided a $162 million
construction loan, which has been fully repaid.
Electrical District No. 5 - Palo Verde Hub
Operational since 2015, this 109-mile transmission line
adds up to 410 megawatts of bidirectional capacity in
Arizona, linking to the vital Palo Verde hub serving
Arizona, Southern California and Nevada. The project is
in active repayment.
TransWest Express
Consisting of an overhead transmission line extending
approximately 725 miles from south-central Wyoming
crossing Colorado and Utah, this project will transmit
about 3,000 MW of electricity annually. TIP provided a
$25 million development loan, which has been
fully repaid.

The Transmission Infrastructure Program’s core
mission is to provide debt solutions to support
the next generation of electric transmission and
domestic energy development. We seek and
provide capital for public-private partnerships to
advance projects that enhance electricity reliability
and affordability and strengthen our nation’s
critical energy infrastructure.
Paul Schwabe, Senior VP & TIP Manager
