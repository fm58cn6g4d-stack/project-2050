U.S. Department of Labor,
Office of Apprenticeship
OUR MISSION
The U.S. Department of Labor’s Office of Apprenticeship promotes and oversees quality Registered
Apprenticeship opportunities for workers seeking higher-skilled, higher-paying jobs and engages
employers seeking to build a qualified workforce.

OUR TEAM AND WHAT WE DO
The Office of Apprenticeship oversees the National Registered Apprenticeship System, which
involves employers, industry associations, sponsors, labor, education providers, Apprenticeship
Industry Intermediaries, State Apprenticeship Agencies, and other Registered Apprenticeship
stakeholders. There are Registered Apprenticeship representatives serving in every state across
the country to:
▶

Provide technical assistance and support to Registered Apprenticeship program sponsors—
employers, industry associations, labor unions, education providers, and other organizations
that administer Registered Apprenticeship programs.

▶

Assist organizations interested in developing and maintaining Registered Apprenticeship
programs that meet national standards for quality.

▶

Partner with State Apprenticeship Agencies to assist organizations in getting their existing
program registered by the Department of Labor.

▶

Ensure ongoing quality program standards.

▶

Answer questions about the Registered
Apprenticeship model.

▶

Connect businesses to a network of partners,
including training providers.

▶

Provide training on how to use recruitment
tools available to Registered Apprenticeship
programs and insight on recruitment sources.

▶

Compile and share program resources
and tools.

▶

Advise partners on available funding sources
to support Registered Apprenticeship,
including awarding and providing oversight
for Registered Apprenticeship investments across
the country.
