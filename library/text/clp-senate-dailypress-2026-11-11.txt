4:02 p.m. The Senate stands adjourned until 12:00 noon on Thursday, November 12. Following Leader remarks, the Senate will proceed to Executive Session to resume consideration of the nomination of Gregory Carl Cook to be United States District Judge for the Northern District of Alabama.
Under regular order, at 1:00 p.m., the Senate will proceed to a roll call vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the Cook nomination.
Additional votes are expected during Thursday’s session.
