Types of Assistance (reported by States)
Heating Assistance was provided by 51 states and served 5,028,871 households with estimated average benefits of $380.
Cooling Assistance was provided by 23 states and served 751,119 households with estimated average benefits of $583.
Year-round Crisis Assistance was provided by 29 states and served 626,391 households with estimated average benefits of $673.
Winter Crisis Assistance was provided by 23 states and served 690,348 households with estimated average benefits of $464.
Summer Crisis Assistance was provided by 5 states and served 105,506 households with estimated average benefits of $251.
Weatherization Assistance was provided by 47 states and served 49,793 households.
*Grant recipients may set income-eligibility between 110% of Federal Poverty Guidelines
(FPG) and the greater of 150% FPG or 60% of State Median Income (whichever is greater).
