Bonneville’s Customers
• Consumer Owned Utilities (COUs)

• Direct Service Industries
– Only one left (Port Townsend Paper)

• Sales Outside of the PNW
• Transmission Customers
– Independent Power Producers, Investor-Owned Utilities, COUs
– Network and Point to Point

Bonneville’s Mission
The Bonneville Power Administration’s mission as a public service
organization is to create and deliver the best value for our customers
and constituents as we act in concert with others to assure the Pacific
Northwest:
• An adequate, efficient, economical and reliable power supply
• A transmission system that is adequate to the task of integrating and
transmitting power from the federal and non-federal generating units,
providing service to BPA’s customers, providing interregional interconnections,
and maintaining electrical reliability and stability; and
• Mitigation of the impacts on fish and wildlife from the federally owned
hydroelectric projects from which BPA markets power.

Transmission Expansion
• Energized 6,800 MW of new generation since 2009
– Facilitated by completion of three 500kV lines (Big Eddy-Knight; McNaryJohn Day; Central Ferry-Lower Monumental)

• Powering New Resources
– Interconnecting 1,000-1,500 MW of new generation annually

• Historic Capital Investment
– More than doubling annual grid investments to over $1 billion by the late
2020s

• Grid Expansion and Reinforcement Portfolio (GERP)
– Nearly $8 billion portfolio across 20+ major projects reinforcing the
regional backbone to unlock roughly 10,000 MW of capacity

Grid Access Transformation (GAT)
Modernizing processes to evaluate and expand the Federal Columbia River
Transmission System
1. Transition Planning: Address near-term customer needs and maximize new
transmission service offers.

2. RAPID (Rapid Access Products & Interconnection Delivery): Delivers tailored
solutions for large loads while ensuring existing customers don’t bear the cost.

– SPRINT (Speed to Power Requests for Interim Non-Firm Transmission): An
accelerated open-season model to connect large loads quickly using available capacity.
– PLAID (Paired Load and Integrated Dispatch): A proposed fast-track
interconnection process for load that pairs with a generation resource.
3. Proactive Planning: 10/20 year expansion planning
4. Accelerate Expansion: Customer build option; expanded contractor pool
