Expanding & Modernizing the Electric Grid
​​​​The Transmission Infrastructure Program (TIP) ( PDF 775KB) is a unique federal infrastructure financing program aimed at expanding and modernizing the electric grid. TIP manages WAPA’s statutory $3.25 billion borrowing authority and can make strategic loans to project applicants for qualifying projects and offer a wealth of experience and expertise to address development hurdles when needed. The program’s primary goal is to leverage federal funds and attract private and other non-federal co-investment to support the development of critical transmission and related infrastructure.​
