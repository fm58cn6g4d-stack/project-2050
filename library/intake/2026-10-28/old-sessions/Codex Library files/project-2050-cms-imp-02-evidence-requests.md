October 23, 2026 | Prepared for Espen Swanson | Drafts for review; not submitted

## Purpose and proposed sequence

The immediate objective is to trace one eligibility-system change from approved scope through contract price, program allocation, budget commitment and cash. The public contract records establish parts of that chain. They do not establish the current ONE APD, the price of HLT-1 restoration, unused contract capacity or available funding.

Begin with existing APD/approval and order registers, the latest executed terms, and one complete medical eligibility change file. Request the detailed accounting extract after those identifiers identify the relevant expenditure. The broader records below should be released in stages to control cost. Existing records concerning future years are in scope; the requests do not ask an agency to create a Project 2050 estimate.

Four institutional routes are verified below. The drafts can be pasted into their official forms. The user must supply required contact details and choose any fee cap before submission. No email, form, FOIA request or fee authorization has been sent. There is no response currently pending.

## Intake and decision rules

Record the submitted request ID, exact text, recipient route, date, fees authorized, scope changes, production date and any no-records or withholding statement. Preserve each document's supplied filename, version, signature date, source and hash. Deduplicate an identical APD or contract produced by several custodians while retaining each production reference.

The minimum complete chain needs: a defined comparable scope; a supported incremental quantity or separately priced change; current executed authority; a without-proposal/base-services comparison; approved program allocation and matching treatment; specific appropriation and commitment; acceptance/invoice/payment evidence for an actual observation; and the federal claim/draw link where applicable. A forecast instead needs an evidenced cash profile, clearly labelled as a forecast. Do not describe a contract ceiling as spent, an APD as an appropriation, a price reduction as a cash release, or an unused field as a verified zero.

If the APD or signed order is withheld or unavailable, retain the chain as partial. Public evidence can still establish contract scope, prices, term boundaries, procurement stages and approval references. It cannot resolve the missing federal/state share, unused capacity or FY2029 cash. Any resulting HLT-1 estimate remains a proposal for review before fiscal export.
