# The network ARCA builds

Three snapshots of American intercity passenger rail: **2036**, the end of ARCA's five-year authorization plus one construction cycle; **2045**, when the corridors it funds are open; and **2055**, when the second generation that the FY2036 reauthorization starts is running. Dates assume the 2026 permitting law and a 2029–30 reconciliation package that puts planning and capital money in the ground before ARCA is written. Eleven corridors carry a green casing: these are not in the FRA pipeline at all, and reach the map only through section 311's federally initiated track. *Alignments are schematic, drawn city-to-city rather than to survey.*
