# What the Renewal Act keeps running, and what it builds

Three snapshots of American transit capital at the same horizons as the rail map: **2036**, the end of the Act's five-year authorization; **2045**, when the projects it started are open; and **2055**. Each dot is one project, sized by cost and placed at its metropolitan area. A green ring marks a system whose operating gap and repair backlog the Act carries. Diamonds are the five terminals that ARCA funds and commuter trains share. *Positions are schematic: dots sit beside their city, not on an alignment.*
