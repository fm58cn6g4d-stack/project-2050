# Sixty-four projects and thirteen systems, twenty years

Every project on FTA's Capital Investment Grants dashboard of September 11, 2026, the eight already building under a signed agreement, and nine that are outside the federal pipeline today, tracked year by year through four policy eras: **BUILD America 250-Plus**, the **2029–30 reconciliation** packages, the **American Transit Renewal Act**, and the **FY2036 reauthorization**. Below them are thirteen systems whose problem is the operating budget, on a much faster clock. For projects, cell color is the federal review stage, the amber bar is construction, and the red edge is service running. For systems, cell color is how the operating gap is being covered, the amber bar is backlog retirement money flowing, and the red edge is a federal service floor in force.

## What the model says

Project names, sponsors, costs, grant requests, phases and estimated grant dates for the 47 pipeline projects are from FTA's Capital Investment Grants Dashboard of September 11, 2026. Grant years here are the dashboard's estimate plus a slip of two years for New Starts over $1 billion and one year otherwise, reflecting FY2027–28. The eight projects building under signed agreements and the nine outside the pipeline were checked against sponsor and press sources on October 5, 2026; years are fiscal years. For the nine, grant, construction and service years are assumptions set later than sponsor targets, and the West Seattle, Ballard and Sepulveda costs are in constant dollars. System gaps are from agency and press sources cited in the bill architecture document. Stages from FY2029 for systems assume the reconciliation transition grants and, from FY2031, the operating tier in section 201.
