# PROJECT 2029: MASTER GOVERNANCE CALENDAR
## A Unified Timeline for the First Democratic Term (2029–2033)
### Version 1.0 — March 2026 | Structural Planning Document

---

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

This calendar synthesizes the legislative, executive, and institutional timelines from all Project 2029 chapters into a single governing timeline. It answers the question that no individual chapter answers alone: *what is happening, across all domains, in any given week or month of the first term?*

It is organized in five phases:
- **Pre-Inauguration** (November 2028–January 20, 2029)
- **The First 100 Days** (January 20–April 29, 2029)
- **Year 1 Completion** (May–December 2029)
- **Years 2–3** (2030–2031)
- **Year 4 and the 2032 Defense** (2032–January 2033)

For each phase, the calendar shows: legislative actions, executive actions, institutional rebuilding, campaign/political milestones, and accountability markers.

## PHASE 0: PRE-INAUGURATION (November 6–January 19, 2029)
*~74 days. The governing plan either succeeds or fails here.*

### Week 1-2 (November 6-19): Transition Launch
**Immediate priorities:**
- Stand up full transition operation within 48 hours of election
- Convene Transition Policy Councils for each domain (Healthcare, Immigration, Climate, Economic Fairness, Democracy/Corruption, International, Civil Service)
- Begin systematic inventory of all Trump executive orders (230+): categorize by Day 1 rescission, review-and-modify, or retain
- Activate pre-drafted EO library for OLC review
- Begin formal damage assessment task force across all agencies

**Personnel:**
- Identify and begin vetting of 500+ transition staff and nominees
- Prioritize the 50 most urgent Senate-confirmed positions for immediate nomination
- Recruit experienced career civil servants for immediate reappointment at gutted agencies

### Week 3-6 (November 20–December 14): Legislative Pre-Coordination
**With Senate and House leadership:**
- Brief incoming Senate leadership on four-reconciliation-bill strategy and sequencing
- Negotiate pre-agreement on filibuster carve-out vote timing and procedure
- Identify the most conservative Democratic senator(s) and begin direct engagement on Restoration Bill
- Establish legislative calendar for first session of 119th Congress
- Prepare draft legislative text for Restoration Reconciliation Bill with CBO pre-scoring

**Agency preparation:**
- HHS/CMS: prepare operational plan for Day 1 Medicaid pause and ACA enrollment reopening
- DHS: prepare immigration enforcement priority guidance
- OPM: prepare Schedule F rescission implementation plan
- DOJ: prepare AG independence norms guidance
- State/USAID: prepare humanitarian aid restart protocols

### Week 7-10 (December 15–January 11): Final Pre-Inauguration Sprint
**Legislative:**
- Lock in commitments from all 50-52 Democratic senators on Restoration Bill (no floor vote without commitment)
- Pre-negotiate any rate/provision modifications with most conservative member
- Draft and pre-file all companion bills for Day 1 EOs

**Nominations:**
- Submit first tranche of 50 nominees to Senate within 5 days of inauguration
- Prepare recess appointment list for positions requiring immediate filling

**Communications:**
- Finalize "First 100 Days" public communications plan
- Prepare agency-by-agency restoration announcement sequence
- Pre-brief key stakeholders: hospital associations, advocacy groups, state governors, union leadership

### Inauguration Week (January 14-20):
- **January 14**: Final OLC review of all Day 1 EOs complete
- **January 18**: New Congressional session convenes; leadership elections
- **January 19**: All Day 1 EO signing events pre-briefed and staged
- **January 20**: INAUGURATION — Day 1 begins

## PHASE 1: THE FIRST 100 DAYS (January 20–April 29, 2029)
*The window of maximum political capital. Front-load everything irreversible.*

### Week 1 (January 20-26): The First Week
**Day 1 (January 20):**
- Tier 1 EOs signed (EO-1 through EO-5): Healthcare enrollment, Schedule F rescission, IG reinstatement, Paris rejoining, DACA restoration
- Day 1 press events: White House signing ceremony; HHS enrollment announcement with real patients; OPM press conference on civil service restoration
- Tier 2 EOs signed throughout the day across agencies

**Day 2-7:**
- Tier 3 EOs (Medicare, HCBS, international, enforcement priorities)
- Submit first 50 nominations to Senate
- ACA special enrollment period opens publicly
- CMS sends formal guidance to all 50 states on Medicaid work requirement pause
- Resettlement organizations notified: refugee contracts restored; funding released
- National Health Service Corps emergency expansion recruitment opens

**By End of Week 1:**
- All Day 1 EOs operational and transmitted to agencies
- AG independence guidance issued
- DOGE forensic audit initiated by relevant IGs
- State Department notifies Paris Agreement secretariat

### Week 2-3 (January 27–February 9): Legislation Introduction
**Legislative:**
- Day 8: Introduce Healthcare and Tax Restoration Reconciliation Bill (FY2029)
- Day 8: Simultaneous introduction of all major companion bills (IG Act, Civil Service Act, Presidential Accountability Act, DREAM Act, Climate Restoration Act)
- Day 10: Begin Senate Finance Committee markup of Restoration Bill
- Day 14: Filibuster carve-out vote introduced and scheduled

**Executive/Administrative:**
- Days 8-14: Tier 4 EOs (EO-56 through EO-78)
- DOJ Government Integrity Unit formally constituted; first briefing to Congress
- OPM begins systematic audit of personnel actions 2025-2029
- USAID begins emergency restart of halted programs

**Political:**
- President travels to rural hospital in a state where closures have been most visible
- Cabinet secretaries deploy to their home-state communities for "restoration listening tours"

### Week 4-6 (February 10–March 2): The Filibuster Vote and Restoration Bill Progress
**Legislative:**
- **Week 4-5: FILIBUSTER CARVE-OUT VOTE** — hold the vote; win or lose, put every senator on record; majority leader statement announces outcome publicly
  - If carve-out passes: immediately introduce John Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act; committee markup by end of Week 6
  - If carve-out fails: announce publicly which senators voted no; redirect strategy to reconciliation maximalism; commit to returning in 2030 with stronger caucus
- Senate Finance Committee completes markup of Restoration Bill; floor scheduling begins
- Begin bipartisan negotiations on immigration reform; set 6-month target for floor vote

**Executive:**
- EPA begins formal rulemaking to restore methane and clean car standards
- FDA reconstitutes advisory committees; issues scientific integrity guidance
- CDC reconstitutes ACIP and all disbanded advisory committees

### Week 7-10 (March 3–March 30): Restoration Bill Floor Action
**Legislative:**
- **Senate floor vote on Restoration Bill** — target: by Day 60 (March 20)
- Vote-a-rama: prepared amendments for all Republican attacks; maintain bill integrity
- House receives and passes Restoration Bill; target: Day 70 (March 30)
- President signs Healthcare and Tax Restoration Act — public signing ceremony

**What the Restoration Act accomplishes when signed:**
- ✅ OBBBA Medicaid work requirements repealed; 11.8 million coverage restorations begin
- ✅ ACA enhanced subsidies made permanent; 5 million regain coverage pathway
- ✅ TCJA reversed for income >$400K; corporate rate to 28%
- ✅ Medicare PAYGO override: $500B in automatic Medicare cuts prevented
- ✅ SNAP restored: ~$175B in food assistance reinstated
- ✅ Child Tax Credit expanded and made permanent
- ✅ Rural hospital stabilization fund funded

### Week 11-14 (March 31–April 29): First 100 Days Completion
**Legislative:**
- Senate Judiciary Committee begins Supreme Court ethics hearings
- Senate HELP Committee begins hearings on AmeriCare public option
- Senate Finance begins hearings on climate/IRA restoration provisions
- Bipartisan immigration reform negotiations active

**Nominations:**
- Target: 100 Senate-confirmed positions filled by Day 100
- Use recess appointments for critical positions where Senate is delaying

**Accountability:**
- Day 100: First public "100-Day Report Card" released — with specific metrics on: coverage restoration numbers, enrollment statistics, federal employees reinstated, IGs operational, DOGE audit status, EOs enacted, nominations confirmed
- Healthcare accountability dashboard goes live (uninsured rate, rural hospital status, drug prices)

## CRITICAL PATH ANALYSIS: THE THREE THINGS THAT CAN DERAIL EVERYTHING

If the plan fails, it will most likely fail because of one of these three choke points:

**1. The Restoration Bill doesn't pass (most dangerous scenario)**
- Cause: A single Democratic senator defects before a commitment is locked in
- Prevention: Pre-negotiate every provision before floor vote; identify the fallback rate/version for every contested element; never go to the floor without 50 committed votes
- If it happens: go back immediately with a narrower bill (healthcare only; leave tax provisions for next vehicle); do not accept failure as permanent

**2. The filibuster carve-out fails and voting rights cannot pass**
- Cause: 1-2 institutionalist Democratic senators refuse to reform the filibuster
- Prevention: Make carve-out commitment a 2026/2028 campaign condition; elect pre-committed senators
- If it happens: pursue every budget-adjacent voting rights provision via reconciliation; partner with state attorneys general on litigation against suppressive laws; make the blocking senators' names famous

**3. The wealth tax is struck down by SCOTUS**
- Cause: Conservative supermajority rules 2% net wealth levy is an unconstitutional "direct tax"
- Prevention: Build the strongest possible legislative record; pursue mark-to-market capital gains as primary alternative; begin constitutional amendment process in parallel
- If it happens: Immediately introduce and pass via reconciliation a mark-to-market capital gains realization regime (which is on unambiguously stronger constitutional ground); the revenue gap is bridged and the policy goal is substantially preserved
