## Independent MA check and qualification

Social Security Act section 1852(a)(5), 42 USC 1395w-22(a)(5), addresses certain significant-cost legislative benefit changes between annual announcements. Unless law requires otherwise, the change does not enter MA contracts until the first contract year following the relevant period. CMS Chief Actuary analysis is part of this determination. Section 1854(a)(1)(A) sets the first-Monday June submission deadline; section 1853 and current CMS materials identify the rate-announcement cycle.

Crucially, contract inclusion and beneficiary access are different. Section 1852(a)(5)(B) suspends the usual MA payment exclusivity for the specified additional benefits during the relevant period. Current 42 CFR 422.109(c)(1) provides direct Original-Medicare payment before adjusted MA contracts take effect. Paragraph (c)(2) preserves important plan responsibilities, including benefits already owed as supplemental benefits and certain diagnostic/follow-up services; beneficiary coinsurance also matters. Paragraph (b) treats changes below the significant-cost threshold differently.

Thus the handoff supports a serious implementation issue, but does not establish that only new mid-cycle MA legislation can permit January-2030 beneficiary access. Review the existing fee-for-service bridge before making that stronger claim. This is a source-based qualification, not a finding that the bridge is sufficient or a decision to adopt it. Significance determination, full benefit scope, supplier/claims readiness, cost sharing, supplemental overlap and funding remain unresolved.
